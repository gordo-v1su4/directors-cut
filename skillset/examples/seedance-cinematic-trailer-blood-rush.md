# Blood Rush Seedance Trailer Example

This is a reference example of a strong concept-only Seedance cinematic trailer prompt.

It is intentionally kept outside `SKILL.md` so the active skill does not overfit to this specific story, setting, title, or rhythm.

## Source Concept

Vampire college series, Twilight meets Euphoria.

## Example Prompt

```text
Create a 12-second cinematic teaser trailer for "BLOOD RUSH," a supernatural college drama where immortality spreads through campus nightlife like a beautiful disease.

Concept: a freshman is drawn into a beautiful, dangerous clique whose parties are not just parties; they are rituals, recruitments, and feeding grounds.

Visual style: neon dorm bathrooms, wet asphalt, fluorescent hallways, moonlit rooftops, smeared eyeliner, glass reflections, glitter on skin, blood glowing under UV light, wealthy campus architecture turning predatory after midnight. 16:9 anamorphic, premium live-action, ARRI Alexa-style, shallow focus, oval bokeh, cinematic halation, realistic motion, no cartoon language.

Pacing: dreamy dread that detonates into savage trailer rhythm. Most cuts are 0.3-0.7 seconds after the opening. Use hard smash cuts, whip-pans, match cuts, black-frame impacts, flash frames, and one sudden silence before the title.

[0:00-0:03 - OPENING HOOK]
One beautiful student stares into a dorm bathroom mirror as the fluorescent light flickers. Her reflection smiles half a second late.

[0:03-0:07 - ESCALATION]
Rapid montage: red cups spilling in slow motion, pupils widening, a rooftop kiss, teeth grazing skin, a hallway sprint, party strobes, a hand dragging bloody fingers across white tile, a professor looking terrified.

[0:07-0:10.5 - PEAK]
The tempo detonates. The clique moves through the party like predators. Flash-frames: eye, mouth, hand, neon, blood, black. The camera does not observe; it gets dragged through the scene.

[0:10.5-0:12 - FINAL STING]
Hard cut to black for one breath. Silence. A close-up of the freshman's eyes turning red. Title card: BLOOD RUSH. Sub-bass hit. Blackout.

Audio direction: warped pop vocal, sub-bass heartbeat, party bass muffled through walls, glass clinks, sudden inhale, wet impact, silence before title.

Rules: no exposition, no generic stock footage, no flat lighting, no slow fades, no overlong shots, no disconnected scenes. Every cut should either reveal story, increase danger, deepen emotion, or hit a beat.
```
