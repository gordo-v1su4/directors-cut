# Video Haven Continuity Test

Four sequential 15-second clips test Kai and Mara across one continuous after-hours scene. Generate only after both audition clips and the Video Haven environment sheet pass review.

## Continuity reference roles

- Character sheet: definitive face, body, apparent age, hair, wardrobe, and accessories.
- Approved audition: definitive voice, vocal texture, cadence, speech rhythm, and restrained performance behavior.
- Environment sheet: definitive Video Haven architecture, geography, signage, props, palette, and practical lighting.
- Previous continuity clip: immediate starting pose, spatial placement, lighting state, props, eyelines, screen direction, and emotional temperature.

Do not let the audition-room background influence Video Haven, and do not let the environment sheet redesign either character.
