## Nano Banana Pro prompt

Image 1 establishes Kai Santana's definitive identity. Image 2 establishes Malik Vale's definitive identity. Image 3 establishes the definitive location and lighting continuity. Preserve all three exactly. After establishing them, refer to the characters only as Kai and Malik.

Create one full-bleed 3x3 grid of nine distinct live-action cinematic frames forming one continuous supernatural pursuit. Thin dark gutters only. No white canvas, outer margin, title, captions, labels, numbers, arrows, or info card. Preserve exact identities and coherent screen direction in every frame.

Shot progression: 1) low macro angle as Malik lands hard in a puddle, water exploding toward lens, fear briefly visible before he hides it; 2) tight moving profile as Kai lands beside him almost silently and clocks Malik's fear; 3) handheld two-shot as a sound behind them makes both turn, Kai irritated rather than frightened; 4) wide lateral composition as Malik accelerates toward the first gap and Kai follows; 5) long-lens side silhouette of both crossing the alley in one impossible floating leap, athletic and predatory rather than superhero-like; 6) low landing angle as Kai skids, catches Malik by the arm, and turns the near-fall into a dry shared laugh; 7) compressed close two-shot behind a parapet as Malik reveals a small amber vial and Kai's charm disappears; 8) deep-focus frame as a distant unseen pursuer crosses the roof behind them and Kai silently orders Malik onward; 9) heroic-but-human final wide as they drop from the last roof toward the unseen Video Haven entrance below, Malik urgent, Kai checking his cheap digital watch because he is late for work.

Camera language: Super 35, 40mm anamorphic base with motivated 65mm close-ups and one 28mm landing shot, controlled handheld urgency, plausible spatial continuity, restrained motion blur, wet reflections, green fluorescent edge light, sodium amber, bruised magenta, realistic skin, subtle grain and halation. The supernatural movement is fast, graceful, and slightly too weightless, never flying with arms extended. No extra people, duplicated characters, identity drift, costume drift, visible fangs, gold eyes, capes, superhero poses, wire-fu stiffness, daylight, clean dry surfaces, modern skyline, text, logos, montage collage, illustration, or fake letterboxing.
