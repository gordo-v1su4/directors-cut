## Nano Banana Pro prompt

Image 1 establishes Kai Santana's definitive identity. Image 2 establishes Malik Vale's definitive identity. Image 3 establishes the definitive Video Haven location and lighting continuity. Preserve all three exactly. After establishing them, refer to the characters only as Kai and Malik.

Create one full-bleed 3x3 grid of nine distinct live-action cinematic frames forming one continuous after-hours handoff at Video Haven. Thin dark gutters only. No white canvas, outer margin, title, captions, labels, numbers, arrows, or info card. Preserve exact identities, room geography, props, lighting, and coherent screen direction in every frame.

Shot progression: 1) wide from the entrance as Kai works alone behind the counter while rain reflections move across the glass; 2) medium counter shot as Kai gives an unseen departing customer a charming apologetic smile and quietly forgives a late fee; 3) rack focus from Kai to the rear staff door opening one inch, his smile remaining in place while his eyes sharpen; 4) narrow back-room two-shot as Malik slips inside rain-soaked and winded, trying to look invulnerable; 5) tight insert as Malik places the amber vial inside an open VHS clamshell and Kai closes it immediately; 6) close fluorescent two-shot as Malik admits something went wrong and Kai's anger flashes before concern wins; 7) reaction close-up as Kai notices Malik's torn sleeve, reaches toward him, then covers the concern with an annoyed look; 8) the front-door bell interrupts, both men instantly reset, Malik disappearing behind the door as Kai lifts the VHS case like ordinary returns work; 9) medium hero shot behind the register as Kai faces the unseen customer with effortless charm while his other hand keeps the closed VHS case out of sight below the counter.

Performance direction: Kai is funny because he keeps ordinary customer-service composure during a real crisis; Malik is charming because his fearless mask fails only around Kai. Their bond should read as practiced trust with unresolved suspicion.
