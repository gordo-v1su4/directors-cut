## Master prompt

Create a cinematic-photoreal 16:9 landscape production environment sheet for one internally consistent off-campus student rental house during freshman fall 2003. The space should look like an ordinary house party, never a modern club. Use a clean five-image contact-sheet layout plus a small readable info card. Panel 1: wide master from the front door showing the worn living room, staircase fixed on the right wall, dead fireplace and mantel fixed left, rear archway to the cramped kitchen, and bay window toward the porch. Panel 2: medium hero angle from the kitchen archway toward the sofa, CRT television, stereo receiver, floor speakers, and front door. Panel 3: reverse 90-degree geography angle from the stairs proving the fireplace, archway, porch door, and bay window relationship. Panel 4: detail insert of CD binders, burned mix CDs, disposable camera, paper party flyer, red plastic cups, corded game controller, landline base, and a beige desktop computer showing an indistinct AIM-era interface with no readable private messages. Panel 5: identical room late at night after the party, warm tungsten, kitchen fluorescent, CRT flicker, streetlight through blinds, and restrained colored incandescent bulbs; no LED strips. Preserve architecture, furniture, prop placement, and nicotine-cream/dark-wood/faded-olive/burgundy palette across panels. Info card: `OFF-CAMPUS HOUSE PARTY / INT.-EXT. / FALL 2003 / PALETTE: TUNGSTEN, OLIVE, BURGUNDY / KEY PROPS: CRT, CDs, DISPOSABLE CAMERA / RECRUITMENT, STATUS, TEMPTATION.` Premium live-action production design reference, realistic wear, legible panel boundaries, clean background plates, absolutely no people. No smartphones, social feeds, LED strips, flat screens, modern speakers, ring lights, contemporary packaging, nightclub staging, illustration, warped architecture, changed geography, or gibberish text.
