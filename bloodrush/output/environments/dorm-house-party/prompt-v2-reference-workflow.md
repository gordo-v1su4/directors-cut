# Off-Campus Party House — Polished Anamorphic Reference Workflow

This location is off campus. It may be modest and furnished secondhand, but it must remain safe, maintained, and believable—not dumpy, condemned, nicotine-stained, or luxurious.

## Stage A — canonical post-party seed

Create one full-frame 16:9 2K live-action cinematic production still of the empty living room of a safe, ordinary off-campus student rental house near a private Catholic college in coastal California, fall 2003, ten minutes after a small college party. Camera just inside the front door at adult eye height, facing a wide archway into a compact rear kitchen. Fix readable geography: staircase rises along the right wall; intact small fireplace and dark wood mantel on the left; bay window faces the front porch; clean honey-toned oak floor with a few normal scuffs; warm cream plaster; mixed but intact olive and burgundy upholstered seating; wood coffee table; CRT television, VCR, stereo receiver, floor speakers, corded landline, and beige desktop computer. Party traces are modest and believable: several red cups, one open pizza box, burned mix CDs, CD binder, disposable camera, paper flyer, and corded game controller.

Warm tungsten table lamps and porch light mix with kitchen fluorescent, CRT glow, and cool streetlight through blinds; restrained youthful energy without nightclub lighting. Photographed practical location, Super 35 sensor with a 40mm anamorphic cinema lens, natural perspective, gentle horizontal falloff, subtle oval bokeh, restrained practical flare, fine 35mm grain, modest halation, physically plausible fabrics, wood, reflections and contact shadows. Compose for a later 2.39:1 crop while delivering full-frame 16:9 with no black bars.

No people, nicotine stains, grime, wall holes, graffiti, broken furniture, torn sofa, trash piles, mold, water damage, filthy carpet, boarded windows, high-school-party cues, keg spectacle, LED strips, ring lights, flat screens, smartphones, modern speakers, contemporary packaging, nightclub haze, luxury staging, HDR real-estate gloss, concept art, illustration, 3D render, excessive bloom, teal-orange grade, warped architecture, duplicated furniture, gibberish text, or fake letterboxing.
