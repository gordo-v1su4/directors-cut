## Master prompt

Create a cinematic-photoreal 16:9 landscape production environment sheet for one internally consistent location: VIDEO HAVEN, an independent college-town video-rental store in fall 2003. Use a clean five-image contact-sheet layout plus a small readable info card. Panel 1: wide master interior from the glass entrance, showing the checkout counter fixed along the left wall, four low media aisles running front-to-back, and the rear staff door. Panel 2: medium hero angle from behind the counter toward the entrance, establishing that the front door is visible from Kai's register. Panel 3: reverse 90-degree geography angle across VHS and DVD aisles toward the counter and returns bin. Panel 4: continuity detail of the wired barcode scanner, corded landline, VHS clamshell cases, DVD cases, plastic membership cards, handwritten shift sheet, and a crisp neon sign spelling VIDEO HAVEN exactly. Panel 5: the identical store after closing, cool fluorescent tubes mixing with restrained cyan, bruised-magenta, and sodium-amber practical spill. Preserve the same architecture, counter position, aisle count, staff door, prop placement, signage, and faded burgundy/charcoal/cream palette in every panel. The small info card must read: `VIDEO HAVEN / INT. / FALL 2003 / PALETTE: CYAN, MAGENTA, AMBER / KEY PROPS: VHS, DVD, CRT, RETURNS BIN / KAI'S WORKPLACE AND CREW COVER.` Premium live-action production design reference, realistic surface wear, legible panel boundaries, natural 24mm and 35mm lens perspective, clean background plates, absolutely no people. No smartphones, streaming branding, QR codes, LED strips, flat-screen televisions, modern point-of-sale hardware, illustration, warped shelves, inconsistent geography, misspelled signage, or unreadable gibberish.
