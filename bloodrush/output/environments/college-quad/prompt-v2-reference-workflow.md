# College Quad — Private-Catholic Anamorphic Reference Workflow

This workflow replaces the earlier state-college/brutalist direction. The new quad is the campus-wide visual anchor for Blood Rush.

## Stage A — canonical daylight seed

Create one full-frame 16:9 2K live-action cinematic location photograph of an empty, well-funded private Catholic college quad on the coastal California corridor in fall 2003. This is a college for adult students, not a high school, state campus, resort, or abandoned institution. Camera at adult eye height beneath a shaded Spanish Colonial Revival arcade, looking across a manicured central lawn toward a dignified academic hall and a modest chapel tower in the distance. Pale warm stucco, cream limestone trim, clay barrel-tile roofs, dark wood doors, arched colonnades, mature palms and cypress, clipped hedges, stone paths, shaded wood-and-iron benches, one glass-front paper bulletin case with period freshman-orientation notices, one discreet pay-phone alcove, early-2000s bicycles, and a small bronze college seal. Maintained and financially stable with gentle age only: softened stone edges, polished old wood, dulled brass.

Late-afternoon coastal daylight, clean natural midtones, subtle atmospheric depth, restrained warm highlights and cool shade. Photographed practical location, Super 35 sensor with a 40mm anamorphic cinema lens, natural perspective, gentle horizontal falloff, subtle oval bokeh, restrained flare, realistic material texture and contact shadows, fine 35mm grain and modest halation. Compose with lateral space and safe headroom for a later 2.39:1 crop, but deliver full-frame 16:9 with no black bars.

No uniforms or people. No high-school lockers, trophy cases, juvenile posters, brutalist state-campus architecture, graffiti, broken fixtures, grime, decay, luxury-hotel gloss, HDR real-estate look, concept art, illustration, matte painting, 3D render, excessive bloom, teal-orange grade, warped architecture, duplicated arches, gibberish text, or modern technology.

## Stage B — orientation views

Attach the approved Stage A seed as the sole `@Image 1`, named `PRIVATE_COLLEGE_QUAD_CANON`.

Use `@Image 1` as the definitive site plan, architecture, materials, planting, daylight, and object-placement reference. Generate each view separately with identical weather and light:

1. original arcade-to-academic-hall master;
2. academic-hall reverse facing the arcade and lawn;
3. cross-axis view proving bulletin case, pay phone, benches, and chapel relationship;
4. lower three-quarter walkway view with clean performance space for two actors.

Every view is a full-frame 16:9 2K live-action location photograph using restrained 40mm anamorphic character and protected for a 2.39:1 crop. Do not redesign or restage.

## Stage C — blue-hour edit

Attach the approved Stage A seed as the sole `@Image 1`. Keep the exact site plan, architecture, landscaping, objects, and camera position. Change only time and weather to recently rained-on blue hour: warm period path lamps, cool arcade practicals, deep cobalt sky, and restrained reflections on clean stone. No people, supernatural effects, new signs, or modern fixtures.

## Acceptance gate

- unmistakably a well-funded coastal private Catholic college for adults;
- Spanish Colonial Revival academic architecture with restrained Catholic identity;
- manicured and maintained without resort or luxury-advertising gloss;
- practical-location photorealism with restrained 40mm anamorphic character;
- full-frame 16:9, protected for 2.39:1, no baked bars;
- no people, high-school cues, state-campus brutalism, neglect, or post-2003 objects.
