The earlier classroom extraction is superseded as an aesthetic reference because it reads as a municipal high school. Do not attach it to Stage A. It may be consulted only for broad fall-2003 instructional technology.

## Stage A — canonical classroom seed

Create one full-frame 16:9 2K live-action cinematic production still of an empty freshman humanities seminar room at a well-funded private Catholic college on the coastal California corridor in fall 2003. This must read unmistakably as a college room for adult students, never a high school. A maintained 1930s academic building renovated in the late 1990s: warm cream plaster walls, tall divided-light windows, dark stained oak trim and doors, polished wood floor, one long U-shaped arrangement of substantial oak seminar tables with exactly 18 separate adult chairs and generous legroom, no tablet-arm desks. At the open end of the U: a large clean green chalkboard, pull-down projection screen, oak lectern, overhead projector on a rolling cart, CRT television with VCR, analog wall clock, corded beige phone, paper syllabi, hardcover course readers, spiral notebooks, and No. 2 pencils. One small tasteful wooden crucifix hangs high beside the chalkboard and one discreet framed college mission notice; Catholic identity is present but not theatrical.

Soft coastal daylight mixes with clean maintained fluorescent practicals, producing skin-friendly neutral midtones and realistic window exposure. Camera from the rear corner at adult eye height. Photographed practical location, Super 35 sensor with a 40mm anamorphic cinema lens, natural perspective, gentle horizontal falloff, subtle oval bokeh, restrained highlight halation, fine 35mm grain, physically plausible materials and contact shadows. Compose for a later 2.39:1 crop while delivering full-frame 16:9 with no black bars.

No people or uniforms. No rows of school desks, tablet-arm chairs, lockers, homeroom signs, juvenile posters, trophy cases, tiny furniture, cafeteria furniture, cinderblock bunker, institutional grime, broken blinds, water stains, graffiti, cracked tile, neglected fixtures, luxury-boardroom styling, modern smartboard, flat screen, smartphone, post-2003 laptop, LED lighting, HDR real-estate gloss, concept art, illustration, 3D render, excessive bloom, teal-orange grade, warped table geometry, duplicated chairs, gibberish text, or fake letterboxing.
