## Casting read

Elias is 20, newly turned, watchful, and volatile. He shares freshman year with his 19-year-old brother after losing a year. His authority is real but brittle; every correction from Rowan touches shame he cannot admit.

## Stage 1 menu

### Role options

- **Older brother losing authority** — Prove love, shame, and command colliding in one restrained exchange.
- **Volatile new vampire** — Prove bodily pressure without relying on fangs or rage.
- **Protective supporting lead** — Prove active listening and fear for someone else.
- **Unreliable witness** — Prove that he believes his own incomplete version of events.

### Audition lines

Line 1: I lost a year. I didn’t lose the right to look out for you.

Line 2: Stop talking to me like you already know how this ends.

Line 3: I am trying very hard not to make this your problem.

### Voice triggers

Voice Trigger A: defensive, protective, ashamed

Voice Trigger B: exhausted, volatile, pleading

Voice Trigger C: authoritative, wounded, restrained
