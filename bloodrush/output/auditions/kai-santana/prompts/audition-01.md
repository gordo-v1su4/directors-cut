## Casting read

Kai is a magnetic 19-year-old former high-school football star whose confidence arrives before his plan. He is street-smart, socially quick, protective, and academically insecure. His mask is playful command; his wound is the fear that charm is the only reason anyone follows him.

## Stage 1 menu

### Role options

- **Lead / reluctant crew authority** — Prove charm, improvisation, and command while the plan visibly forms in real time.
- **Campus social king** — Prove that people trust him before they notice he is lying.
- **Protective friend under pressure** — Prove fear and loyalty without surrendering his confident mask.
- **Dangerous rule keeper** — Prove threat through lowered volume rather than aggression.

### Audition lines

Line 1: I said I’d handle it. That is not the same thing as having a plan.

Line 2: You can hate me tomorrow. Tonight, you need to listen.

Line 3: Smile. If they think we’re having fun, they stop asking questions.

### Voice triggers

Voice Trigger A: amused, cornered, quietly commanding

Voice Trigger B: protective, breathless, improvising

Voice Trigger C: charming, ashamed, defiant

Kai has promised to contain a serious problem, but the reader has realized he is improvising. He begins listening with a crooked, easy smile, chin slightly raised, as if their concern is almost funny. The reader challenges him. His smile holds for one beat too long; he glances aside, takes a small breath, then returns direct eye contact. Amused, cornered, quietly commanding, he says exactly: “I said I’d handle it. That is not the same thing as having a plan.” Stress “handle it,” then let “having a plan” land with honest self-awareness.

Silence. His jaw firms and the charm becomes authority without a raised voice. He gives one tiny nod that means the conversation is over. Hold two seconds after the dramatic beat. Then he breaks naturally: the pressure leaks out as a small involuntary laugh, he looks toward the reader and softly says, “Sorry,” still recognizably Kai rather than playing a joke. Keep speech intelligible and lip sync precise.
