## Casting read

Kai is a magnetic 19-year-old former high-school football star who can sell confidence before he has a plan. His street intelligence is real; his academic confidence is mostly performance. The retake must preserve the easy charm, the sudden authority, and the natural end break while letting casting read his eyes.

## Stage 1 menu

### Role options

- **Lead / reluctant crew authority** — Prove that charm can harden into command without losing likability.
- **Campus charmer caught improvising** — Prove fast social intelligence and honest self-awareness.
- **Protective liar** — Prove that he can conceal fear while trying to keep someone safe.
- **Former athlete without a playbook** — Prove confidence, shame, and adaptability under pressure.

### Audition lines

Line 1: I said I— I said I’d handle it. That is not the same thing as having a plan.

Line 2: You keep calling it confidence like I didn’t work this hard to look calm.

Line 3: I can get us out. I just need everybody to stop asking where we’re going.

### Voice triggers

Voice Trigger A: snapping, checked, quietly commanding

Voice Trigger B: cocky, protective, improvising

Voice Trigger C: embarrassed, defiant, unexpectedly tender
