## Casting read

Lucian is a watchful, volatile 19-year-old newly changed vampire. He uses cool distance to keep other people safe. Mara’s fear produces unwanted tenderness because he recognizes it in himself.

## Stage 1 menu

### Role options

- **Newly changed confidant** — Prove recognition, intimacy, and danger held inside restraint.
- **Volatile supporting lead** — Prove that stillness is an active effort not to lose control.
- **Unwanted romantic possibility** — Prove attraction without glamour posing or certainty.
- **Loyal outsider** — Prove that trust is a choice made against instinct.

### Audition lines

Line 1: I know what you’re hearing. The part I can’t explain is why it chose you.

Line 2: Don’t come closer. I’m not asking twice because I want to scare you.

Line 3: You think I’m calm because you can’t hear what I’m trying not to do.

### Voice triggers

Voice Trigger A: intimate, afraid, restrained

Voice Trigger B: cold, protective, breathless

Voice Trigger C: volatile, tender, ashamed
