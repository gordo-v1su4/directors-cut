## Casting read

Malik is a guarded, fearless 21-year-old night runner. His body is always prepared to move, but his social weapon is an audacious cover story delivered like the listener is already on his side.

## Stage 1 menu

### Role options

- **Streetwise supporting lead** — Prove fast social calculation without caricature.
- **Courier with divided loyalty** — Prove that protecting information costs him personally.
- **Cornered rival** — Prove humor becoming danger when the lie fails.
