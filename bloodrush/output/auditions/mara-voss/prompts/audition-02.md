## Casting read

Mara is a vigilant 19-year-old freshman whose instinct is to observe before she reacts. Her public mask is composure; under pressure she becomes more precise. This retake must show distinct emotional range inside one continuous exchange while keeping her visibly human.

## Stage 1 menu

### Role options

- **Lead / human resisting supernatural gaslighting** — Prove fear, intelligence, anger, and agency in one escalation.
- **Suspicious outsider** — Prove active listening and the ability to expose an evasive answer.
- **Tragic investigator** — Prove that curiosity continues after the truth becomes frightening.
- **Newly endangered witness** — Prove a shift from self-doubt to certainty without melodrama.

### Audition lines

Line 1: My reflection moved first. Stop telling me I’m tired, and tell me what you did.

Line 2: I know what I saw. The part I don’t know is why you’re lying about it.

Line 3: You keep asking me to calm down like calm is going to make this less true.

### Voice triggers

Voice Trigger A: stunned, suspicious, furious

Voice Trigger B: frightened, analytical, defiant

Voice Trigger C: intimate, betrayed, controlled
