## Casting read

Mara is a vigilant 19-year-old freshman whose instinct is to contain fear and observe. Her mask is control; her wound is the belief that needing help gives someone else power over her. The audition must prove that terror makes her more precise, not passive.

## Stage 1 menu

### Role options

- **Lead / human at the edge of transformation** — Prove intelligence, fear, and agency inside an impossible bodily change.
- **Suspicious outsider** — Prove active listening and the ability to expose a lie without becoming shrill.
- **Newly awakened threat** — Prove that control can be more frightening than rage.
- **Tragic investigator** — Prove curiosity that continues even when the answer may destroy her.

### Audition lines

Line 1: Tell me why my reflection looked away before I did.

Line 2: I remember the music. I remember the door. I don’t remember saying yes.

Line 3: If you’re trying to calm me down, start by telling the truth.

### Voice triggers

Voice Trigger A: frightened, analytical, fiercely controlled

Voice Trigger B: exhausted, suspicious, precise

Voice Trigger C: intimate, angry, disbelieving
