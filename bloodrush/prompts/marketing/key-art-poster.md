# BLOODRUSH — Key Art Poster Prompts

## Poster v1 — Tight medium, three leads (2:3)

**References:** Image 1 = title typography style; Image 2 = cast; Image 3 = moodboard (cool teal-blue palette)

```text
MAIN KEY ART POSTER for "BLOODRUSH," set in 2003. A hyper-stylized vampire college drama — freshman rush, pledging, initiation.

COMPOSITION: Tight medium shot, not full-body. The three lead guys fill the frame from roughly mid-thigh up — no stretching, natural proportions. Kai center, two others flanking close to him. Image 3 is the moodboard reference for the feel.

SCENE: Kai "Hoodie" Santana center-frame — bleached blonde curly hair, open gray hoodie over bare chest, light jeans, white sneakers, silver wraparound shades pushed up, glowing amber eyes, fangs visible. Left: shirtless muscular mohawk guy, glowing yellow eyes, black pants, dark necklace. Right: dark-skinned guy with glowing yellow eyes, black jacket over maroon hoodie, black pants. All three stand close together, facing camera, slightly angled toward each other. Wet asphalt below reflects the blood moon. Solo cups scattered on ground. Fog/smoke drifts at their feet.

BACKGROUND: College quad at night — brutalist concrete buildings, silhouetted palm trees. A massive textured blood moon hangs in the upper left. Red vertical banners: "WELCOME FRESHMEN 2003" and "PLEDGE BLEED BELONG." Warm amber light from building windows.

COLOR PALETTE: Cool teals and deep blues as the base (from Image 3 moodboard), contrasted with blood red sky, glowing yellow eyes, amber window lights. Dark, moody, supernatural-horror. Not warm magenta.

STYLE: Y2K-era cinematic movie poster, 2003, shot on film. ARRI Alexa, anamorphic, crushed blacks, cool teal-blue grade with red accents. Professional Hollywood film poster, high contrast, dramatic, photorealistic. Natural human proportions — no vertical stretching.

TYPOGRAPHY: "BLOODRUSH" in large chrome serif letters at the bottom third, metallic silver with weathered pitted texture, sharp serifs, blood dripping from the bottom edges, one droplet sliding down the R, fractured glitch effect. Above: "FRESHMAN YEAR IS HELL." Below: "THIS FALL 2003." Studio logos at very bottom: Warner Bros, Touchstone, Dimension Films.

LAYOUT: Vertical poster 2:3. The three guys tightly framed from mid-thigh up, occupying the center 50-60% of frame. Title at bottom 25%. Tagline above title. Natural proportions.
```

## Poster v2 — Walking toward camera (2:3)

```text
VERTICAL MOVIE POSTER for "BLOODRUSH." Dark, moody, supernatural aesthetic dominated by deep reds and blacks.

FOREGROUND: Three young men walk toward the viewer on a wet, reflective asphalt street.

CENTER FIGURE: Kai "Hoodie" Santana — bleached blonde curly hair, open gray hoodie over bare chest, light-wash baggy jeans, white sneakers, silver wraparound shades. Confident, stoic, intense expression. Fangs visible. Main character.

LEFT FIGURE: Blonde hair, pale skin, red and black jacket over gray tee, gray jeans, black and white sneakers. Head slightly bowed, face in shadow. Exhaling smoke/vapor.

RIGHT FIGURE: Dark skin, short dark hair, black jacket over maroon hoodie, black pants, black boots. Glowing yellow vampire eyes. Sharp fangs. Intense expression.

All three wear silver cross necklaces. Image 2 is cast reference — Kai 1st from left, blonde 3rd, dark-skinned 4th.

BACKGROUND: Large brutalist concrete building with warm orange-lit windows. Palm trees frame scene. Massive glowing red blood moon behind center character's head. Blood-red sky with swirling clouds. Wet asphalt reflects red sky and amber streetlamp. Red vertical banners: "VERIDIAN UNIVERSITY" with large "V."

STYLE: Shot on film, anamorphic, crushed blacks, high contrast, deep crimson and black palette, photorealistic. Professional Hollywood movie poster.

TYPOGRAPHY: Top in small white serif: "WELCOME TO FRESHMAN YEAR." Title "BLOODRUSH" in large metallic silver 3D lettering, bladed edges, red blood dripping from letters. Below in small red serif: "THIS FALL." Studio logos: A24 (left), NEON (center), FOCUS FEATURES (right). Title style matches Image 1.

LAYOUT: Vertical 2:3. Three figures walk toward camera — Kai center-front, others flanking. Title bottom 30%. Natural proportions, no stretching.
```
