# BLOODRUSH — Key Art Poster Prompts

> **Status:** Merged into fall 2003 canon.

## Variant B — Crimson/black, walking toward camera

```text
VERTICAL MOVIE POSTER for "BLOODRUSH." Dark, moody, supernatural aesthetic dominated by deep reds and blacks.
FOREGROUND: Three young men walk toward the viewer on a wet, reflective asphalt street.
CENTER FIGURE: Kai "Hoodie" Santana — bleached blonde curly hair, open gray hoodie over bare chest, light-wash baggy jeans, white sneakers, silver wraparound shades. Confident, stoic, intense expression. Fangs visible. He is the main character.
LEFT FIGURE: A young man with blonde hair, pale skin, wearing a red and black jacket over a gray t-shirt, gray jeans, black and white sneakers. His head is slightly bowed, face partially in shadow. Exhaling smoke/vapor.
RIGHT FIGURE: A young man with dark skin, short dark hair, wearing a black jacket over a maroon hoodie, black pants, black boots. Glowing yellow vampire eyes. Sharp fangs visible. Intense expression.
All three wear silver cross necklaces. Image 2 is the cast reference — Kai is 1st from left, the blonde is 3rd, the dark-skinned guy is 4th.
BACKGROUND: Large brutalist concrete building with warm orange-lit windows. Tall silhouetted palm trees frame the scene on both sides. A massive glowing red blood moon hangs in the sky directly behind the center character's head. The sky is a dramatic blood-red with swirling clouds. Wet asphalt street reflects the red sky and warm amber streetlamp light. Two red vertical banners on streetlamps: "VERIDIAN UNIVERSITY" with a large "V."
STYLE: Shot on film, anamorphic, crushed blacks, high contrast, dramatic lighting, deep crimson and black palette, photorealistic. Professional Hollywood movie poster.
TYPOGRAPHY: At top in small white serif: "WELCOME TO FRESHMAN YEAR." The title "BLOODRUSH" in large metallic silver 3D lettering with sharp bladed edges, red blood dripping from the bottom of the letters, especially the R, U, S, H — a large pool of blood sliding off the R. Below in small red serif: "THIS FALL." Studio logos at very bottom in red: A24 (left), NEON (center), FOCUS FEATURES (right). The title style matches Image 1.
LAYOUT: Vertical poster 2:3. The three figures walk toward camera in a triangular formation — Kai center-front, the other two slightly behind and flanking. Title at bottom 30%. No stretching, natural proportions.
```
