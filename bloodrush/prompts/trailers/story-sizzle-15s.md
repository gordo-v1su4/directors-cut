## Final Seedance prompt

Create a 15-second cinematic sizzle trailer for **BLOOD RUSH**, a fall-2003 supernatural college drama about young vampires passing as ordinary freshmen until a human girl is turned without permission and begins crying black blood.

Use Images 1–3 as strict character identity locks and Images 4–7 as strict location locks. Preserve them exactly across every cut. Do not describe, redesign, restyle, recast, age, or replace the referenced characters. Keep ordinary human-looking eyes except during one motivated flash-frame. Use the original trailer's Neon Gothic momentum, but every image must now belong to this story.

Format: full-frame 16:9 protected for a later 2.39:1 crop, no baked black bars. Premium live-action, Super 35, 40mm anamorphic base, motivated 65mm macro inserts, controlled handheld urgency, realistic motion and physics, shallow focus only when emotionally useful, oval bokeh, restrained horizontal flares, cinematic halation, fine grain. Rain-polished pavement, green fluorescent light, cyan night ambience, sodium amber, bruised magenta, glass reflections, CRT flicker. Faces remain readable and human.

Pacing: savage and escalating. Most cuts last 0.3–0.7 seconds, tightening to 0.2-second flash-frames at the peak. Hard cuts, whip-pans, match cuts, one black-frame impact, one sudden silence before the title. Only one slow-motion supernatural movement beat.

**0:00–0:02.7 — THE RUN**  
Black. A shoe detonates a rooftop puddle. Malik lands hard; Kai lands beside him almost silently. Both turn toward an unseen pursuer. Low lateral tracking as they sprint and cross an alley in one fast, graceful, slightly too-weightless leap. Kai lands, checks his digital watch, and gives one irritated half-smile: he is late for work. Smash cut on impact to the Video Haven door chime.

**0:02.7–0:05.8 — THE COVER**  
Kai is already behind the Video Haven register, effortlessly charming an unseen departing customer. He slides the late-fee slip aside as if granting a favor. Rack focus: Malik slips through the rear staff door. Macro insert: the amber vial disappears inside a VHS clamshell. Kai closes it and locks the front door. His smile vanishes.

**0:05.8–0:09.2 — THE BREACH**  
Match cut from the vial's amber glass to Mara's eye in the campus bathroom mirror. Fluorescent buzz. Mara grips the sink, fighting panic. Lucian stops in the doorway and recognizes what she cannot see. One black blood-tear forms beneath Mara's eye. The music cuts to total silence for one breath.

**0:09.2–0:12.8 — THE GROUP FRACTURES**  
The tempo detonates. Rapid story flashes: Kai staring at the black tear, charm gone; Rowan facing Elias in a blocked doorway, love turning into rage; Mara and Lucian inches apart but not touching; a gold eye reflected in a dark classroom window; Malik looking back across a wet roof; the amber vial rolling across the Video Haven counter; a house-party strobe catching one hungry face; Kai slamming the VHS case shut. Flash-frames: puddle, eye, vial, cross, fang, black tear, door lock.

**0:12.8–0:15 — THE QUESTION / TITLE**  
Near silence. Tight close-up of Kai looking just off lens at Mara, frightened but already taking control. He says, low and exact: **“Who turned her?”** Hard black-frame impact. Title slams: **BLOOD RUSH** — chrome serif, restrained fractured glitch, one dark droplet moving down the R. Sub-bass hit. Final sound: the Video Haven door chime ringing once in darkness.

Audio: warped pitched-down dreampop vocal over a distorted trap pulse, sub-bass heartbeat, rooftop impacts, rain, fluorescent hum, VHS clamshell snap, store bell, one vacuum of silence before Kai's line, one bass hit on the title.

Rules: no invented lead characters, no generic coven lineup, no motel, no dive bar, no modern phones, no texting, no public-school or high-school imagery, no uniforms unless already present in a definitive reference, no unexplained bodies, no random gore, no costume drift, no identity drift, no repeated faces, no superhero flight, no flat lighting, no slow fades, no exposition cards, and no text except the final title. Every cut must reveal story, increase danger, deepen emotion, or hit the rhythm.
