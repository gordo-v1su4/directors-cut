## Clip 2 — Bathroom Panic (9s)

**Function:** Flickering dorm bathroom; mirror reflection lags; black tear appears.

```text
Camera: ARRI Alexa Mini, Anamorphic, oval bokeh, stretched flares, strong highlight bloom | cool-toned digital texture with pronounced highlight bloom and horizontal anamorphic flare streaks | 40mm, f/2.0. Camera Style: Aggressive erratic handheld with one whip-pan snap and one brief macro intrusion, no slow motion, no smooth stabilized glides, no perfectly locked symmetry. Light: Hostile diegetic sources — buzzing fluorescent ceiling panel top-down, mirror-side strip underlighting the face, doubled highlights in the glass and chrome, corners dropping toward blue-black, no daylight. Style & Mood: Neon-Gothic split-tone: bruised magentas, cyan/morgue-blue, sodium-amber pockets, kept cool overall. Late-night dorm sterility that feels unsafe, with the fluorescent buzz as a pressure layer. Acting: micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise. Narrative Summary: Lead Girl fights to keep control at a dorm sink as the infection shows itself in the mirror and a black tear appears. Scene Setup: Dorm bathroom — narrow tiled room with a long sink counter and wall mirror, chrome faucets, paper towel dispenser, stall doors deeper in the room, a floor drain near the sinks, damp smears on tile. Buzzing fluorescent ceiling panel plus mirror-side strip light create harsh doubled highlights. Camera 8 feet behind and slightly left of Lead Girl, eye-level, 40mm wide framing that includes her hunched at the sink and her face in the mirror; camera faces the mirror wall, stalls sit behind her. Lead Girl's hands are already gripping the sink rim; faucet is off; no items in her hands; exactly one Lead Girl. First frame state: eyes molten gold; no tear streak; register: infected shock. Dynamic Description: 24mm wide, eye-level, erratic handheld; the flickering fluorescent and mirror strip strobe across wet porcelain as Lead Girl hunches over the sink, knuckles locked on the rim, eyes molten gold and wide, breath trying to slow but catching. Hard Cut to 90mm macro; her fingers crush the sink edge, nails biting, a tiny tremor running through her hand as the fluorescent buzz spikes. Hard Cut to 50mm mirror-first MCU; her reflection holds her molten-gold stare, jaw slightly slack, then lags half a second behind as she starts to turn. Whip-pan from her lagging mirror image to her real face as she completes the turn, the framing snapping onto her actual eyes. Hard Cut to 85mm tight insert at the sink line; her grip clamps harder, tendon in her wrist jumps once, and her breathing stops for a beat. Hard Cut to 75mm CU, eye-level on her cheek and eyes; a single black tear of blood swells at the lower lid and tracks down her cheek in a slow, unbroken line as she freezes, stunned — brow cinches, a slow blink sticks halfway, throat works once, the face of someone watching a dark stain spread through clean fabric; her breath releases in short, panicked pulls while the black streak remains visible. Audio: Fluorescent hum and intermittent buzz, distant dorm plumbing, a faint water drip in the room, cloth rustle as she braces, tight uneven breathing, small ceramic creak under her grip. Positive Locks: Lead Girl remains a late-teen girl with shoulder-length dark hair and pale skin, eyes molten gold, hunched at the sink. The black tear streak is a single dark track down her cheek by the end of the clip. Cool Neon-Gothic split-tone persists with bruised magentas and cyan/morgue-blue under hostile fluorescent light. Same dorm bathroom geometry with the long mirror, sink counter, chrome faucets, and stall doors in the background. No subtitles. No text overlay. No captions. No title cards. No watermarks.
```

## Clip 3 — Coven Montage (13s)

**Function:** Beat-drop montage — neon bar, motel snarl, party fangs, rooftop sprint, moon leap.

```text
Camera: ARRI Alexa Mini, Anamorphic, oval bokeh, stretched flares, strong highlight bloom | cool-toned rendering, bruised neon bloom, halation on hot highlights, visible grain | 40mm, f/2.0. Camera Style: Aggressive erratic handheld with violent whip-pans used sparingly for impact, occasional slow-mo float on apex moments, occasional macro intrusions, no perfectly stable frame, no locked-off serenity, no gentle gliding. Light: Hostile diegetic sources only — neon sign magenta/cyan bands raking faces from the side, toxic green spill from bar interior backlighting hair, sodium streetlamp amber pockets from above, cigarette glow points, VACANCY sign red-pink with audible buzz, strobe bursts and UV party glow, cold city spill on wet concrete, darkness between sources falling to near-black. Style & Mood: Neon-Gothic split-tone: bruised magentas and cyan/morgue-blue with sodium-amber pockets; cool night drizzle, sweaty haze, unsafe faces under underlight and backlight. Staccato beat-drop montage escalation, physical and predatory. Acting: micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise. Narrative Summary: Lead Girl pulls Boy 1 and Boy 2 into a beat-drop montage that turns panic into predatory exhilaration, ending with her launched against the moon on a rooftop. Scene Setup: Outside a neon-soaked dive bar at night — wet asphalt reflecting magenta/cyan bands, door cracked open spilling toxic green light and cigarette smoke, puddles stippled by light drizzle, distant city glow beyond. Camera 10 feet from the bar door, low-angle wide, 28mm anamorphic. Lead Girl stands centre-frame at x≈50% y≈70%, Boy 1 flanks screen-left at x≈28% y≈72%, Boy 2 flanks screen-right at x≈72% y≈72%; all three face toward camera with the bar door behind them. Camera shoots from the street side; the Boy 1→Lead Girl→Boy 2 left-to-right order holds and the camera stays on one side of the line. Hands empty; no props in hand. Exactly one Lead Girl, one Boy 1, one Boy 2. Dynamic Description: 28mm low-angle wide three-shot, aggressive handheld. Neon magenta/cyan stripes crawl across the wet street while cigarette smoke spills through the bar door behind them; Lead Girl's eyes are molten gold and a black tear streak cuts down her cheek, her breath fast, brows pinched, jaw trembling like she is trying not to bolt. The beat drops and her posture changes in one visible snap — shoulders square, chin lifts, mouth stills into a thin line; the same molten-gold stare turns forward with predatory exhilaration as Boy 1 and Boy 2 lock in on either side. Hard Cut to 50mm medium shot in a motel corridor under a buzzing VACANCY sign — Boy 1 shirtless, chest wet with something dark, lips peel back to show gold fangs; his nostrils flare and his eyes burn molten-gold as he inhales through his teeth. Whip-pan from the strobe-writhing house party crowd to Lead Girl's mouth in macro close-up. Under UV flashes, her upper lip lifts and the gold fangs descend toward exposed skin; her lower eyelids tighten and her jaw sets with the look of someone finally choosing the bite over the fear. Cut on contact to 35mm wide tracking on a parking-garage rooftop — wet concrete, cold city spill, sodium pools — the three sprint at 45 km/h, motion-blurred, feet slapping water into fans as the inhuman speed compresses the distance in two strides. Hard Cut to 24mm low-angle wide; Lead Girl launches impossibly high in one explosive push, the camera tilts up and floats into slow motion as she rises past the roofline into the open night. The moon fills the sky behind her at peak height; she hangs for a breath, hair and clothing snapping in the drizzle, head tilting down toward the rooftop with a small, controlled mouth-corner lift as her chest rises once, adrenaline visibly riding the stillness. Audio: Neon buzz and transformer hum, bar-door bass thump muffled by smoke, drizzle tapping concrete, strobe-synced party kick, a sharp whip-pan whoosh, wet foot slaps and spray, wind at rooftop height, a brief breath-held hush at the apex. Positive Locks: Lead Girl keeps molten-gold eyes and the black tear streak; Boy 1 keeps gold fangs and molten-gold eyes; Boy 2 stays the third flank in the coven trio. Boy 1 screen-left, Lead Girl center, Boy 2 screen-right whenever they share the frame, with the camera held on one side of the line. Neon-Gothic split-tone remains consistent: bruised magentas, cyan/morgue-blue, sodium-amber pockets in cool night air. The montage stays in a cohesive night urban world: neon dive bar exterior, VACANCY-lit motel corridor, strobe house party, and wet parking-garage rooftop leading to the moonward apex. No subtitles. No text overlay. No captions. No title cards. No watermarks.
```
