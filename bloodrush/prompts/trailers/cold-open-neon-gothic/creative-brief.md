# BLOODRUSH — Cold Open Teaser (Neon Gothic)

**Genre:** Hyper-stylized vampire college drama — Euphoria neon intimacy × Twilight supernatural yearning × Gen-Z coven of pretty monsters.

**Aesthetic:** Neon Gothic — bruised magentas, clinical morgue-blues, sodium-streetlamp amber, wet-glint of blood under fluorescent light.

**Pacing:** Savage. 0.3–0.7s cuts building to strobing 0.2s flash-frames at the drop. Warped pitched-down trap remix of dreampop — reverb female vocal, sub-bass pulse, sudden silence before title card.

**Aspect:** Anamorphic 2.39:1 | ARRI Alexa grain | faces lit from below or behind

---

## Beat Structure

### [0:00–0:03] The Infection

- Slow-motion wide: brutalist college quad at magic hour — three silhouettes toward camera, bleeding red sunset, palm trees
- Hard cut: ECU girl's pupils dilating, iris brown → molten gold, veins blooming at lash-line
- Cut: dorm bathroom, fluorescent flicker, grip on sink, reflection lags half a second
- Single black tear of blood tracks down cheek

### [0:03–0:07] The Coven

- Beat-drop. Tempo detonates.
- Coven emerges from neon dive bar — lead girl center, two boys flanking, electric pink/toxic green backlight, cigarette smoke
- Shirtless boy in motel corridor, chest wet, gold-fanged snarl under buzzing VACANCY sign
- Whip-pan house party — bodies in strobes — CU fangs descending, lips parting in erotic surrender
- Three sprint parking-garage rooftop, inhumanly fast; lead girl leaps impossibly high; camera tilts into moon

### [0:07–0:11] The Ritual

- Vocal sample: *"we were never supposed to live forever—"*
- Ornate amber vial between manicured fingers
- Boy's glowing amber eyes through frosted classroom window
- Girl in pleated skirt over body in parking lot, blood on chin, texting casually
- Coven on rooftop ledge, blood moon, hoods up, ready to drop
- Flash-frames: fang, eye, neon sign, wrist, moon, fang, black

### [0:11–0:12] The Title

- Total silence
- Lead girl — mouth bloody, gold eyes wet — stares into lens
- Whispered: *"welcome to freshman year."*
- Title slams: **BLOODRUSH** — chrome serif, fractured glitch, blood droplet on R
- Subsonic bass hit. Black.
