# Neon Gothic — Visual Style Lock

> **Status:** Extracted from development dump. Dating says **2001** — align to **2003** and current cast in `docs/series-bible.md` where applicable.

A neon-gothic fever dream of bruised pinks and icy blues where every face feels lit like a secret being exposed, and blood glints wetly in harsh, clinical glow. The camera moves like a hungry witness—jerky and relentless—until it suddenly goes still, trapping you in her stare.

## Approved parameters

- Aspect: 2.39:1 anamorphic
- Grade: bruised magentas, cyan/morgue-blue shadows, sodium-amber pockets
- Camera: erratic handheld → dead-still final stare
- Faces: lit from below or behind — never safely
- Motif: molten-gold eyes + black tear; flicker/buzz → silence contrast
- Controlling idea: belonging as contagious gaze — panic becomes craving
