# BLOODRUSH — Video Haven: Location Bible
**Address:** 2147 Veridian Avenue, corner of Palm Drive. A low-slung brick building with a faded red awning, sandwiched between a laundromat and a Thai restaurant that's been "coming soon" since 1997.
**Established:** 1978 — bought by David Santana's father. David and Linda took over in 1995.
**Status:** A legitimate family business. A video store. A place where the community rents movies, buys candy, and chats about the new releases. Also: a distribution hub for the only thing keeping a campus full of vampires from ripping each other's throats out. Kai's parents don't know about the second part.
---
## 1. THE SANTANA FAMILY
### David Santana (Kai's father, born 1955)
David grew up in Veridian. He worked at Video Haven as a teenager, took over from his father in 1995. He's a practical man — doesn't believe in ghosts, vampires, or anything that goes bump in the night. He believes in inventory, late fees, and the importance of rewinding. He's the kind of dad who wears a "BE KIND, REWIND" t-shirt unironically. He loves his son. He doesn't understand him.
David knows the store has strange customers. He knows some tapes go missing and reappear. He knows the back room sometimes smells different. He chalks it up to college kids and old plumbing. He's not curious. He's too tired to be curious.
### Linda Santana (Kai's mother, born 1958)
Linda is the heart of the store. She runs the front counter, remembers every customer's name, and stocks the candy display. She makes a mean pot of coffee and keeps a stash of homemade cookies under the counter for regulars. She knows Kai is drifting. She doesn't know how to reach him. She buys him a new hoodie every Christmas — he always picks the gray one.
Linda has noticed that the store's after-hours business has picked up. She's found empty vials in the return slot. She thinks they're from the smoothie shop next door. She throws them away. She doesn't ask questions.
### Kai "Hoodie" Santana (born 1983)
Kai works the closing shift — 9 PM to midnight. He's the only employee his parents trust to lock up. He doesn't know the store is a front. Not at first. But he's noticed things. The tapes that get returned without being rented. The customers who come in right before closing and stand in the horror aisle without browsing. The amber vials he finds in the return slot, in the back room, behind the counter.
He starts collecting them. He doesn't know why. He doesn't know what they are. But he's been carrying the first one he found for weeks, and he hasn't told anyone.

## 2. THE STORE — Full 2001-Era Inventory
### Exterior
- **The sign:** A large rectangular neon sign mounted above the entrance — "Video Haven" in red cursive script, illuminated from within. The "V" flickers when the weather's humid. The "n" has been dim for three years. David says he'll fix it next month. He's been saying that since 1998.
- **The windows:** Two large display windows flanking the glass door. Current display: a cardboard standee for *The Mummy Returns* (2001), a faded *The Matrix* poster (1999) behind it, and a hand-written sign: *"BE KIND, REWIND — $0.50 fee for unrewound tapes."*
- **The door:** A glass door with a brass handle. A decal on the glass reads "31" — the street number. Bells jingle when it opens. The sound is distinctive — a high-low chime. Kai hears it in his sleep.
- **The awning:** Striped red and white, faded to pink in places. It's about 12 feet off the ground. In the rain, it creates a dry pocket. The coven stands under it. They never enter together.

### The Elixir — How the Distribution Works
The amber vials are a **synthetic blood substitute** — the elixir — that suppresses vampire hunger. The Council controls the supply. The vials are distributed through a network of "neutral ground" locations across campus. Video Haven is one of them.
**The system:**
1. A courier (Sloane) drops a fresh supply of vials into the storage room cache every Sunday night, after closing.
2. Coven members and other affiliated vampires pick up their weekly supply by leaving an empty vial in a designated drop box (the hollowed *Lost Boys* case or the foreign section empty cases) and collecting a full one from the cache.
3. The exchange happens during regular business hours, hidden in plain sight. A vampire rents a movie. They browse the foreign section. They swap the vials. They leave.
4. **Kai's parents have no idea.** David found a loose vial once. He thought it was a weird lip gloss. Linda threw it away.
5. **Kai starts noticing in Episode 1.** He finds the first vial in the *Lost Boys* case. He starts watching the pattern. He starts collecting the empties. He doesn't know what he's holding. But he knows it's important.
### The Regulars

- **The Coven:** They don't rent. They *lurk.* They stand in the horror aisle, talking in code, watching the door. They pick up their vials and leave. They never buy anything. They never leave empty-handed. David thinks they're "those goth kids from the art department."

## 3. THE BACKSTORY — Mr. Yoshida and Yuki
### Kenji Yoshida (born 1946)
Kenji Yoshida immigrated from Osaka in 1965. He was 19 years old. He had $200, a suitcase, and a letter of acceptance to Veridian University. He studied biology. He was the first person in his family to go to college.
In 1969, he was a graduate student studying the campus's unique cave system. He found the tree. He found the bones. He found the Council. They offered him a choice: forget what he saw, or join them. He refused both. He walked away. He never published his findings.
He got a job at the Registrar's Office. He married. He had a daughter. He lived a quiet life. He never stopped watching.
### Yuki Yoshida (born 1980)
Yuki grew up in Veridian. She was a curious child — always asking questions, always poking into things she shouldn't. Her father told her stories about the caves beneath the campus. She was fascinated. She was 17 when she found the entrance herself.
She didn't tell her father. She went down. She found the tree. She touched it. The tree chose her. The Council bound her.
She became a **conduit** — a living bridge between the tree and the Council's operations. Not a vampire. Not human. Something in between. Her body is alive, but her *self* is tethered to the tree. She can walk the campus. She works at the Registrar's Office, the same building as her father. She sees him in the hallway sometimes. She never speaks to him. She's not allowed to.
### The Connection to Video Haven
Mr. Yoshida started coming to Video Haven in 1986. He noticed the store's strange after-hours traffic. He noticed the hollowed tape cases. He noticed the amber vials.
He's been watching the store for 15 years. He knows it's a distribution hub. He knows the Santanas don't know. He's been waiting for the right person to discover the tunnel. He's been waiting for Kai.
He's not a threat. He's a witness. And one day, he'll be an ally.

## 4. THE HIDDEN TUNNEL ACCESS
### Location
The tunnel entrance is inside the **storage room** of Video Haven. The room is behind the back office, past a narrow hallway lined with boxes of inventory. The door is painted the same color as the wall (a faded beige), making it nearly invisible.
### The Entrance
The entrance is hidden **beneath a loose floorboard** in the storage room, behind the heavy shelving unit of dead stock. The floorboard is splintered along one edge — the only sign it's been lifted. Underneath: a trapdoor with an iron ring. The trapdoor is old, heavy, sealed with a padlock that looks like it's been there for a century.
**The padlock** is a Yale & Towne from 1890. It's rusted, antique, and keyed to a skeleton key. Kai finds the key in Episode 1 — it's tucked behind the loose floorboard, taped to the underside. He doesn't know what it opens. He keeps it.
### The Staircase
Beyond the trapdoor, a narrow staircase descends into the earth. The walls are unfinished stone, sweating with condensation. The stairs are steep, uneven, worn smooth by what looks like centuries of use. The air gets colder with every step. The hum gets louder.
There are **47 steps.** Kai counts them every time. The 47th step opens into the cave system.
### The Cave System
The tunnel connects to a **natural limestone cave network** beneath Veridian University. The caves are ancient — the university's founding documents mention them, but the Council sealed all public access in the 1920s.
The route from Video Haven to the tree chamber:
1. **Video Haven storage room** (47 steps down)
2. **The Junction** — a fork in the tunnel. Left leads to the Greek Row basements. Right leads to the tree chamber. Straight leads deeper, to a place Kai never explores.
3. **The Bone Gallery** — a narrow passage where the walls are embedded with human bones. The Council's dumping ground from the 1800s.
4. **The Anteroom** — a small chamber before the tree chamber. A stone altar. Candles. A single amber vial, always full, always waiting. The Council's offering station.
5. **The Tree Chamber** — the cavern. The tree. The roots. The bones.
### Who Knows
- **Kai's parents** — don't know. The storage room is David's least favorite part of the store. He never goes in there.
- **The Council** — knows the tunnel exists. They don't know the Santanas are oblivious. They're fine with it.
- **The Coven** — knows about the tunnel. Uses it when they need to. They don't know about the floorboard. They enter through the Greek Row entrance.
- **Mr. Yoshida** — knows about the tunnel. Found it in 1969. Has never told anyone. He's been waiting.
- **Kai** — doesn't know yet. He has the key. He's felt the draft. He's heard the hum. He'll find it in Episode 7.

## 5. SEVEN KEY SCENES AT VIDEO HAVEN
### Scene 1: "The First Night" (Episode 1, Act One) — Yuki's Influence
**Setting:** The store is closing. 10:45 PM. The neon sign flickers. The last customer leaves. Kai is at the counter, rewinding tapes. The bell on the door jingles.
**The Coven enters.** All seven of them. They move like they own the place. Mara leads — she walks straight to the horror section, not browsing, just *standing.* The others fan out. Blaise starts flipping through the new releases, making comments to nobody. Dare goes to the foreign section. Rourke stands by the door, arms crossed. Sloane disappears into the back aisles. Vic stares at the Staff Picks wall.
**Yuki's presence (unseen):** Across the street, in the shadow of the laundromat awning, Yuki Yoshida watches. She's been standing there for an hour. She's the one who told Mara about the new kid at Video Haven. She's the one who said: *"He's like me. He doesn't know it yet. But he will."*
**Kai's POV:** He watches them on the security feed. They don't rent anything. They don't talk to each other. They're waiting. After exactly 11 minutes, Mara walks to the counter. She places a single tape on the counter: *The Lost Boys.* "I'd like to rent this."
Kai: "That's a classic."
Mara: "So are we."
She pays with cash. She leaves without the tape. Kai looks down — the tape is still on the counter. Underneath it: a small amber vial. He pockets it before David comes back from the break room.
**What Kai doesn't know:** The tape case is hollowed. Inside the sleeve, slipped behind the cover art, is a folded piece of paper. A handwritten note in neat, precise script: *"Don't drink it. Keep it. Show it to the old man at the foreign section. — Y.Y."*
Kai doesn't find it until closing. He's checking the tape — it's already rewound, but he notices the case is heavier than it should be. He opens it. The note falls out. He reads it. He looks at the amber vial. He looks at the foreign section. He doesn't know what it means yet. But he knows the name.
**Across the street:** Yuki watches Kai read the note. She watches him look up, confused, toward the foreign section. She smiles. It's the first time she's smiled in three years. She turns and walks back into the night.
**The sound:** The doorbell jingles. The coven is gone. The neon sign buzzes. Kai stares at the note in his hand.

### Scene 2: "The Old Man" (Episode 3, Act Three) — Mr. Yoshida Speaks
**Setting:** After closing. Midnight. The store is dark except for the neon glow through the windows. Kai is at the counter, organizing the return slot. The bell jingles. Mr. Yoshida walks in.
Kai: "We're closed."
Mr. Yoshida: "I know."
He walks to the foreign section. He doesn't browse. He waits. Kai watches him on the security feed. After a long moment, Kai walks over.
**The conversation:**
Kai: "You're Mr. Yoshida. You leave the cards."
Mr. Yoshida: "You found the note."
Kai goes still. "How do you know about the note?"
Mr. Yoshida: "Because I wrote it."
Kai: "The note said 'Y.Y.' — that's you?"
Mr. Yoshida: "It's my daughter. Yuki. She wrote the cards. She wrote the note. She's been watching you for weeks."
Kai: "Why?"
Mr. Yoshida: "Because she knows what you're about to become. And she wants to help you."
Kai: "What am I about to become?"
Mr. Yoshida is silent for a long time. He looks at the horror section. At the *Lost Boys* case. At the empty place on the shelf where the hollowed case sits.
"You're about to find out that the world is bigger than this store. Bigger than this street. Bigger than anything your parents ever told you. And when you do, you're going to need someone who's been there."
Kai: "And that's you?"
Mr. Yoshida: "That's Yuki."
**The beat:** Mr. Yoshida places a small key on the shelf. An old key. A skeleton key. "The storage room. Behind the dead stock. There's a loose floorboard. Lift it. You'll find the door. I opened it once, in 1969. I've been too afraid to open it again."
Kai: "What's down there?"
Mr. Yoshida: "The truth. And my daughter."
He walks to the door. He pauses. "Don't tell your parents. They're good people. They don't need to know."
The bell jingles. He's gone.
**Outside the window:** Yuki watches from the shadow of the awning. She sees her father leave. She sees Kai standing in the foreign section, holding the key. She presses her palm against the glass. Just for a moment. Then she's gone.

### Scene 3: "The War Council" (Episode 8, Act Three) — Yuki Returns
**Setting:** The store is closed. The blinds are drawn. The neon sign is off for the first time in years. The coven is gathered in the horror aisle. The Alpha Delta house has joined them. The store is packed — 15 vampires in a video store, all of them armed, all of them hungry.
**The atmosphere:** Tension. The air is thick. The vampires haven't fed in days. The elixir is gone. The Council cut the supply. The hunger is a living thing in the room. David and Linda are at home, asleep. They don't know their store has been turned into a war room.
**The argument:**
Blaise (pacing): "This is insane. We're going to war with a council that's been running this campus for 150 years. With what army? A bunch of broke vampires from the art house?"
Dare (quiet): "We have the tunnel. We have the tree. We have the element of surprise."
Blaise: "And what happens when we get to the tree? We tap it? We don't even know how."
Mara: "We learn."
Kai: "We have Yuki."
The room goes silent.
Kai: "Mr. Yoshida's daughter. She's bound to the tree. She's been a conduit for three years. She knows how it works. She knows how to tap it. And she knows how to burn it."
Mara: "She's also a volunteer. The Council doesn't control her. She's been waiting for a chance to destroy them."
Blaise: "And you trust her?"
Kai: "I trust that she wants the Council dead more than we do."
**Yuki enters.** The bell on the door jingles. Every vampire in the room turns. She's standing in the doorway — pale, thin, dark circles under her eyes. She's wearing a black coat over a Registrar's Office blouse. She looks like a ghost. She *is* a ghost. She's been dead to this world for three years.
**The moment:**
She walks to the foreign section. She touches the index cards in her own handwriting. She hasn't seen them in three years. She turns to face the coven.
"The tree is alive. It's been alive longer than this campus. It doesn't want to be fed. It wants to be *free.* The Council has been forcing it to feed on death for centuries. If we tap it, we don't just take the elixir. We *free* it. And when it's free, it will choose who to give its sap to. Not the Council. Not the Dean's Office. *Us.*"
Blaise: "And you know this how?"
Yuki: "Because I've been inside it. For three years. I've seen what it remembers. I've seen what it *wants.* And I've seen what happens if we don't do this."
She looks at Kai. "This store is the last place the Council will look. It's been under their nose the whole time. That's why it's the perfect place to start."
**The sound:** The neon sign flickers once, then holds. The coven starts to move. They're ready.

### Scene 4: "The Night Before" (Episode 10, Act One) — Yuki's Goodbye
**Setting:** The night before the assault on the tree. The store is closed. The coven is scattered around the store — some watching *The Craft* on the break room TV, some sleeping on the couch, some standing in the horror aisle, staring at the shelves. Nobody can sleep.
**The quiet moment:**
Kai is at the counter, alone. He's holding the amber vial from Episode 1. He's never opened it. He's been carrying it for weeks.
Mara walks up. She stands beside him. She doesn't look at him.
"You're not going to drink it."
Kai: "I don't know."
Mara: "You won't. It's not who you are."
