# Blood Rush

Blood Rush is a one-hour supernatural college drama set in fall 2003. Kai "Hoodie" Santana, a charming former high-school football star with more street sense than academic discipline, keeps a volatile group of young vampires hidden inside ordinary freshman life. When Mara Voss is turned without permission and manifests black blood-tears tied to a rare bloodline, the group's rules—and Kai's control—begin to fracture.

The characters should pass as normal 2003 college students during the day. The visual language becomes Neon Gothic only at night, during hunger, transformation, and secrecy failures. The tone favors supernatural yearning, identity, friendship, and dry humor over conspicuous goth styling or constant horror spectacle.
