# Blood Rush — Cold-Open Tests

## Recommended opening: The late shift

Open on movement before explanation. Kai and Malik cross rain-slick college-town rooftops with an amber vial while an unseen pursuer stays one roof behind. The movement is graceful, fast, and slightly too weightless. Kai is more irritated about being late for his Video Haven shift than he is frightened by the pursuit. They drop toward the store.

Smash cut to Kai behind the Video Haven register, perfectly dry in his customer-service manner even if he is physically soaked. He forgives a human customer's late fee because the customer is embarrassed and because the store belongs to his uncle. Malik slips into the rear staff room. The vial is hidden inside a VHS clamshell. Kai's public charm turns into private command, then concern, then instantly back to charm when the front-door bell rings.

This opening promises supernatural movement, Neon Gothic night photography, Kai's humor, Kai and Malik's bond, the suppressant mystery, the 2003 world, and Video Haven's story function before exposition begins.

Kai's sexuality does not need to be announced in the cold open. His social ease and customer-service charm remain genuine rather than heterosexual signaling. The additional secret should enter later through a moment in which his practiced confidence becomes unexpectedly careful around a man he actually wants to know.

## Alternate opening: Mara in the mirror

Open on Mara alone in the campus bathroom after hours. The scene begins as a human panic attack under green fluorescent light and becomes supernatural only when Lucian sees one black tear before Mara does. This is a cleaner audience-entry opening and emotionally intimate, but it delays Kai and makes the pilot initially feel more familiar.

## Current recommendation

Test the rooftop-to-Video-Haven opening first. Keep the Mara bathroom board as the strongest alternate if the supernatural movement feels synthetic in video.

## Video Haven story function

Video Haven belongs to Kai's human maternal uncle, Rafael "Rafa" Santana. Rafa gave Kai the job after Kai's football plan collapsed and college tuition became a family strain. Kai genuinely helps keep the marginal store open and sends part of his pay home. Rafa trusts Kai with closing shifts and the back room.

Kai abuses that trust by using the rear staff room as the crew's after-hours meeting point. It works because the store has late hours, a landline, a rear service exit, physical inventory large enough to hide small objects, and a credible reason for young people to come and go in 2003. The arrangement makes the cover useful but morally expensive: exposure would not only endanger the crew; it would destroy the relative who gave Kai somewhere to land.
