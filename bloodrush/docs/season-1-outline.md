# Blood Rush — Season 1 Outline

> Merged from the PLEDGE development draft into fall **2003** canon (`series-bible.md`, `characters.md`, `pilot-outline.md`).

## Season engine (dual track)

**A — Pilot mystery (Episodes 1–3):** Mara Voss is turned without authorization and manifests black blood-tears. Kai must find who broke the turning rule while containing a co-lead who proves the group's rules are incomplete.

**B — Supply war (Episodes 4–12):** The amber suppressant flows from a living tree beneath campus, controlled by a centuries-old **Council** operating through the Dean's Office. When supply is cut, hunger, betrayal, and the Yuki/tree bond force Kai's crew to choose between obedience and burning the system down.

Both tracks run together: Mara's anomaly is the crack; the Council is the load-bearing wall.

## Three-act structure

| Act | Episodes | Title | What happens |
|-----|----------|-------|--------------|
| **RUSH** | 1–4 | Passing / Breach | Kai navigates freshman life and Video Haven cover; Mara turns; crew containment; first amber vial; Yoshida thread opens; Council presence confirmed |
| **BLEED** | 5–8 | Hunger / Exposure | Supply thins; black tears recur; tunnel and tree revealed; betrayal inside the crew; war council at Video Haven |
| **BELONG** | 9–12 | Choice / Aftermath | Assault on the tree; Yuki's sacrifice; Council overthrown; crew learns to survive without the drug; Dean Vane backup tease |

## Amber vial arc

1. **Ep 1:** Kai finds a vial hidden in a hollow *Lost Boys* case — Yuki's first contact.
2. **Ep 4–5:** Crew dependence on suppressant becomes explicit; scarcity = leverage.
3. **Ep 7:** Kai learns the vial comes from a living tree via the tunnel beneath Video Haven.
4. **Ep 11:** Tree destroyed / freed; Yuki's bond breaks.
5. **Ep 12:** Crew adapts; post-credits: Dean Vane's backup stockpile and label **GRCTR-00 — The Original**.

## Antagonists — The Council (Dean's Office)

Ancient vampires running Veridian like a cartel. **Dean Vane**, **Cross**, and **Finch** control amber distribution, Greek-row recruitment, and the conduit binding Yuki to the tree. They prefer docile hunger to open war.

## Episode sketch (12 × 1 hr)

### RUSH (1–4)

1. **Pilot — The Breach:** Rooftop or campus cold open; Video Haven night shift; Mara turned; black tear; Kai discovers the rule was broken. *(See `pilot-outline.md`.)*
2. **Containment:** Cover story for Mara; amber vial economics; first Owl's Nest meeting.
3. **The Old Man:** Mr. Yoshida after closing; Yuki named; skeleton key to storage-room trapdoor.
4. **Council:** Dean's Office flex; Greek rush as recruitment pipeline; Mara's bloodline questioned.

### BLEED (5–8)

5. **Cutoff:** Supply stopped; hunger symptoms; Elias/Rowan fracture surfaces.
6. **Underneath:** First descent; bone gallery; tree glimpsed.
7. **Roots:** Kai learns amber source; Mara's black tears linked to dormant bloodline.
8. **War Council:** Fifteen vampires in the horror aisle; Yuki returns; Video Haven as last safe staging ground.

### BELONG (9–12)

9. **Army:** Crew builds coalition; Lucian betrayal thread peaks.
10. **Night Before:** Quiet store beat; Yuki's goodbye note on Rafa's monitor.
11. **The Last Light:** Battle at tree; Yuki sacrifices; tunnel collapses.
12. **Aftermath:** Store returns to normal; Yuki's legacy card in foreign section; post-credits Vane stockpile.
