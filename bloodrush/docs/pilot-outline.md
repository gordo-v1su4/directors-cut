# Blood Rush — Pilot Spine

## Pilot promise

The pilot begins with recognizable 2003 freshman life and gradually reveals that Kai is managing a supernatural crisis behind an ordinary campus social world. Mara is turned without authorization; the group must contain her while determining whether one of their own broke the rules.

## Cold open

1. Morning campus: Kai appears like an ordinary, overconfident former athlete navigating freshman orientation. People know him or think they do.
2. In class, the group passes as normal. Tiny mistakes—an overlong stare, a reaction to a paper cut, a reflection held too long—suggest something is off.
3. Mara notices the group's synchronized attention but dismisses it as clique behavior.
4. Night at Video Haven: Kai works for his human uncle Rafa, using charm to manage customers and conceal the crew's back-room activity inside the family business.
5. A mundane interruption hides a supernatural exchange involving the amber vial.
6. Mara leaves a party or late campus event disoriented after an unseen encounter.
7. In the campus bathroom, her reflection lags emotionally rather than literally at first. Her eyes warm toward gold. A black tear appears.
8. Cut to Kai realizing someone has broken the turning rule.

## Act progression

### Act one — Passing

Establish Kai's social power, academic weakness, and Video Haven cover. Mara sees the group as strange but attractive in the ordinary social sense.

### Act two — The breach

Mara experiences sensory distortions and missing time. Elias and Rowan's conflict reveals that the group is already unstable before her arrival.

### Act three — The change

Mara's bathroom transformation forces Lucian to help her. His recognition creates intimacy, but he cannot explain the black tears.

### Act four — Containment

Kai takes control, questions Mara, and tries to determine who had access to her and the suppressant. He presents rules confidently while improvising half of the solution.

### Act five — The lie

The group returns Mara to public life with a mundane cover story. She appears normal enough to pass, but the black tears return in private. The final reveal shows that someone expected this outcome.

## First continuity test

The initial four-clip production test uses Video Haven after closing:

1. Mara enters and tries to conceal the first black tear.
2. Kai locks the door and questions her.
3. The transformation escalates and Kai recognizes an unauthorized turning.
4. A customer tries the door; both instantly behave normally, and Kai dryly reframes the crisis as freshman-year inconvenience.
