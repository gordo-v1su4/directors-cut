# Blood Rush — Location Visual Canon

## Campus identity

The college is a well-funded private Catholic college on the coastal California corridor in fall 2003. It is not a public high school, a neglected state campus, an abandoned institution, or a prestige-resort fantasy. The school has old institutional confidence, maintained grounds, and selective late-1990s renovations layered into older architecture.

The dominant campus language combines restrained Spanish Colonial Revival and collegiate Catholic details: pale warm stucco, cream limestone trim, shaded arcades, dark wood doors, clay barrel-tile roofs, mature palms and cypress, clipped lawns, discreet bronze wayfinding, and a chapel tower or cross visible only when compositionally natural. Midcentury classroom and service wings may exist, but they are clean, maintained, and clearly part of a private college.

Catholic identity should register through architecture and small practical details rather than spectacle: a modest wall crucifix, chapel axis, carved seal, saint-name room plaque, or framed campus mission notice. Avoid giant devotional imagery, gothic-horror staging, clergy extras, or repeated crosses.

Uniforms are not required for the current environment or audition pass. Students may wear believable fall-2003 college clothing.

## Condition and class signals

- Maintained, cleaned daily, and financially stable.
- Mild age is acceptable: polished old wood, softened stone edges, gently dulled brass, repaired grout, a few pinholes in a bulletin board.
- No broken mirrors, graffiti, water stains, cracked fixtures, nicotine-colored walls, filthy grout, vandalized stalls, institutional neglect, or abandoned-building decay.
- No high-school cues: lockers, cafeteria trays, juvenile motivational posters, homeroom signage, trophy cases, or undersized desks.
- No luxury-hotel polish, sterile showroom perfection, or implausibly new 2020s renovation.

## Photoreal capture contract

Every canonical location seed must read as a photographed practical location or constructed live-action set:

- full-frame 16:9 2K delivery, composed with safe headroom and lateral space for a 2.39:1 extraction;
- 40mm anamorphic cinema-lens character on a Super 35 sensor;
- natural perspective, gentle horizontal falloff, subtle oval bokeh, restrained edge distortion, and restrained horizontal flare only when motivated by a practical light;
- no baked letterbox bars, fake film-frame overlays, heavy teal-orange grading, excessive bloom, crushed blacks, HDR real-estate gloss, concept art, 3D rendering, or painterly texture;
- physically plausible materials, shadow contact, reflections, fixture scale, lens depth, and exposure;
- fine 35mm grain, clean midtones, modest halation, and realistic highlight rolloff.

## Location-specific direction

### Campus bathroom

A maintained women’s restroom in a renovated 1930s academic building: warm cream ceramic wall tile, intact dark-stone counter or two porcelain basins, a long clean mirror, brushed chrome fixtures, muted blue-gray stall partitions, intact acoustic ceiling, and soft fluorescent practicals supplemented by warm corridor spill. It can feel private and uncanny without looking dirty.

### Freshman classroom

A real college seminar room for adults, not a high-school classroom: 20–24 movable wood-and-metal seminar chairs, substantial oak instructor table, chalkboard, pull-down screen, overhead projector, CRT/VCR cart, tall windows, cream plaster, dark wood trim, discreet crucifix, and maintained late-1990s lighting.

### College quad

A manicured coastal private-college quad framed by arcades, limestone or stucco academic buildings, mature palms and cypress, clipped lawn, stone paths, shaded benches, a paper bulletin case, pay phone, and chapel tower glimpsed across the axis. Elegant but inhabited, never a resort brochure.

### Off-campus party house

The party house remains off campus and less polished, but it is an ordinary, safe student rental—not a dump. Use maintained plaster, intact floors, mixed secondhand furniture, period electronics, and believable party disorder without grime, wall damage, nicotine staining, or condemned-house cues.

### Video Haven

Video Haven remains a modest independent 2003 video store. Its slight retail wear is intentional and does not establish the school’s class level. Preserve the approved geography while improving only photographic credibility and anamorphic capture language in derived views.
