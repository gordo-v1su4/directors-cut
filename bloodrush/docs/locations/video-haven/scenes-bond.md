# 6. TWO SCENES FROM THE OTHER SIDE OF THE BOND

### Scene 6: "The Conduit" (Episode 5, B-Plot) — Inside the Tree
**Setting:** The tree chamber, beneath Veridian University. The cavern is vast, cathedral-like, lit by the amber glow of the tree's sap. The tree stands at the center — massive, ancient, its bark black and veined with gold. Its roots spread across the floor like the arms of a drowned god, wrapped around the bones of a century of failed pledges.
**Yuki is here.** She's been here for three years. She's not a prisoner — not in the physical sense. She can walk the campus, do her work at the Registrar's Office, move through the world. But she's *tethered.* A root as thin as a finger is wrapped around her ankle, disappearing into the stone floor. She doesn't feel it anymore. She hasn't felt it in years.
**Her perspective:**
The tree doesn't speak in words. It speaks in *memory.* When Yuki is in the chamber, she sees everything the tree has seen — the founding of the university in 1849, the first Council meeting, the first pledge, the first body fed to the roots. She sees wars, plagues, students laughing, students dying, students being dragged into the basement and never coming back. The tree remembers everything. And because Yuki is bound to it, she remembers everything too.
**The moment:**
Yuki sits at the base of the tree. Her back is against the bark. Her eyes are closed. She's not resting. She's *listening.*
The tree shows her a memory: 1969. A young man (Mr. Yoshida, 23 years old) in a graduate student's jacket, holding a flashlight. He's in the caves. He's taking notes. He finds the tree. He touches it. He feels the sap. He runs. But he doesn't forget.
The tree shows her another memory: 1999. Yuki herself, 17 years old, walking down the same stairs. She's not afraid. She's *curious.* She walks past the bones. She touches the tree. The tree reaches for her. She doesn't pull away.
The tree shows her a third memory: *the future.* A boy with bleached blonde hair, standing in a video store, holding a note. Her note. He's reading it. He's looking at the storage room. He's going to find her.
**Yuki opens her eyes.** She's crying. She doesn't know if the tears are hers or the tree's. She whispers: "Is he the one?"
The tree pulses. The amber sap glows brighter. The roots around her ankle tighten, then loosen. A single drop of amber sap falls from a branch above her. It lands on her palm. It's warm. It's an answer.
**The other side of the bond:**
Above ground, at that exact moment, Rafa Santana is closing Video Haven. He flips the sign to "CLOSED." He locks the door. He doesn't know why, but he stops in the foreign section. He looks at the index cards. He reads one — *"The Last Wave* (1977). Water. Everywhere." He doesn't understand it. But he feels a warmth in his chest. He shakes it off. He goes home.

### Scene 7: "The Last Light" (Episode 11, Act Two) — The Other Side of the Glass
**Setting:** The tree chamber, during the battle. The cavern is in chaos. The Council's enforcers are fighting Kai's army. The tree is pulsing, its roots writhing, its amber sap flowing like blood from a wound. The chamber is filled with smoke, fire, and the sound of screaming.
**Yuki is at the center.** She's standing at the base of the tree. Her hands are pressed against the bark. The root around her ankle has grown — it's now wrapped around her entire leg, up to her thigh. The tree is holding her. It doesn't want to let go.
**Her perspective:**
The tree is in pain. For the first time in centuries, it's afraid. The Council has been feeding it death for so long that it's forgotten how to live. Yuki can feel its confusion, its hunger, its desperate need to *survive.* The tree is showing her everything — all at once, like a flood.
She sees the first Council member, a man in a 19th-century suit, making a deal with the tree: *"Give us your sap, and we will feed you."* She sees the tree agree, not knowing what it was agreeing to. She sees a century of bodies. She sees the tree's regret, buried so deep that even the Council doesn't know it's there.
The tree shows her one last thing: *the way out.* How to break the bond. How to set the tree free. How to die.
**The moment of connection:**
Above ground, in the quiet of the storage room, Mr. Yoshida is not fighting. He's standing in the dark, alone. The trapdoor is open. He can hear the battle from below. He's not going down. He's not a fighter. He's a father.
He closes his eyes. He speaks to the air. "Yuki. If you can hear me — it's okay. Let go. I'll be okay."
**And she hears him.** The bond between them — the one that's been stretched thin for three years — snaps into focus. She feels his voice. She feels his love. She feels him letting her go.
**The other side of the bond:**
Yuki opens her eyes. She's crying. She's smiling. She presses her forehead against the tree's bark. "He said it's okay."
The tree pulses. It understands. It doesn't want to lose her, but it understands.
Yuki looks up at Kai, who's fighting his way toward her. He's covered in blood. He's screaming her name. She meets his eyes. She smiles.
"Thank you for finding me."
She turns back to the tree. She places both hands on the bark. She closes her eyes. She starts to *pull.* The tree's amber sap flows into her — not feeding her, but *draining.* She's taking the tree's essence into herself. She's becoming the conduit. She's becoming the sacrifice.
**The tree's gift:**
As Yuki pulls the tree's life into herself, the tree gives her one last memory. A gift. A memory of the world before the Council. A forest at dawn, silver leaves, amber sap flowing freely, birds singing, the sun warm on the bark. The tree, *free.*
Yuki smiles. "I'll take you there."
She pulls harder. The tree starts to wither. The roots retract. The amber sap slows. The chamber starts to collapse.
**Above ground:** Mr. Yoshida feels it. A warmth in his chest, then a cold. He knows. He falls to his knees in the storage room, the trapdoor still open. He doesn't cry. He just stares into the dark.
**The last light:**
Yuki's body is glowing. The amber sap is flowing through her veins, lighting her up from the inside. She looks beautiful. She looks like the girl in the Polaroid.
She looks at Kai one last time. "Tell my father I loved him. And tell him I'm finally free."
She closes her eyes. The amber light flares. The tree crumbles. The chamber collapses. And Yuki Yoshida, the girl who was never meant to survive, becomes the light that saves them all.
**The sound:** A roar. A flash. Silence. Then, far above, the bell on the door of Video Haven jingles once. A gust of wind blows through the store. The foreign section flutters. The index cards shift. The candle on the counter goes out.
Mr. Yoshida looks up. He knows. He closes the trapdoor. He doesn't need it anymore.
