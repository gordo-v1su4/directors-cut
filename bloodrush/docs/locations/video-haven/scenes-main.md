> **Canon:** Fall 2003. Video Haven owned by Rafael "Rafa" Santana (Kai's maternal uncle). Merged from PLEDGE development draft — cast names in scene beats use current bible where noted.

# 5. SEVEN KEY SCENES AT VIDEO HAVEN

### Scene 1: "The First Night" (Episode 1, Act One) — Yuki's Influence
**Setting:** The store is closing. 10:45 PM. The neon sign flickers. The last customer leaves. Kai is at the counter, rewinding tapes. The bell on the door jingles.
**The Coven enters.** All seven of them. They move like they own the place. Mara leads — she walks straight to the horror section, not browsing, just *standing.* The others fan out. Lucian starts flipping through the new releases, making comments to nobody. Rowan goes to the foreign section. Malachi stands by the door, arms crossed. Malik disappears into the back aisles. Elias stares at the Staff Picks wall.
**Yuki's presence (unseen):** Across the street, in the shadow of the laundromat awning, Yuki Yoshida watches. She's been standing there for an hour. She's the one who told Mara about the new kid at Video Haven. She's the one who said: *"He's like me. He doesn't know it yet. But he will."*
**Kai's POV:** He watches them on the security feed. They don't rent anything. They don't talk to each other. They're waiting. After exactly 11 minutes, Mara walks to the counter. She places a single tape on the counter: *The Lost Boys.* "I'd like to rent this."
Kai: "That's a classic."
Mara: "So are we."
She pays with cash. She leaves without the tape. Kai looks down — the tape is still on the counter. Underneath it: a small amber vial. He pockets it before Rafa comes back from the break room.
**What Kai doesn't know:** The tape case is hollowed. Inside the sleeve, slipped behind the cover art, is a folded piece of paper. A handwritten note in neat, precise script: *"Don't drink it. Keep it. Show it to the old man at the foreign section. — Y.Y."*
Kai doesn't find it until closing. He's checking the tape — it's already rewound, but he notices the case is heavier than it should be. He opens it. The note falls out. He reads it. He looks at the amber vial. He looks at the foreign section. He doesn't know what it means yet. But he knows the name.
**Across the street:** Yuki watches Kai read the note. She watches him look up, confused, toward the foreign section. She smiles. It's the first time she's smiled in three years. She turns and walks back into the night.
**The sound:** The doorbell jingles. The coven is gone. The neon sign buzzes. Kai stares at the note in his hand.

### Scene 2: "The Old Man" (Episode 3, Act Three) — Mr. Yoshida Speaks
**Setting:** After closing. Midnight. The store is dark except for the neon glow through the windows. Kai is at the counter, organizing the return slot. The bell jingles. Mr. Yoshida walks in.
Kai: "We're closed."
Mr. Yoshida: "I know."
He walks to the foreign section. He doesn't browse. He waits. Kai watches him on the security feed. After a long moment, Kai walks over.
**The conversation:**
Kai: "You're Mr. Yoshida. You leave the cards."
Mr. Yoshida: "You found the note."
Kai goes still. "How do you know about the note?"
Mr. Yoshida: "Because I wrote it."
Kai: "The note said 'Y.Y.' — that's you?"
Mr. Yoshida: "It's my daughter. Yuki. She wrote the cards. She wrote the note. She's been watching you for weeks."
Kai: "Why?"
Mr. Yoshida: "Because she knows what you're about to become. And she wants to help you."
Kai: "What am I about to become?"
Mr. Yoshida is silent for a long time. He looks at the horror section. At the *Lost Boys* case. At the empty place on the shelf where the hollowed case sits.
"You're about to find out that the world is bigger than this store. Bigger than this street. Bigger than anything your uncle ever told you. And when you do, you're going to need someone who's been there."
Kai: "And that's you?"
Mr. Yoshida: "That's Yuki."
**The beat:** Mr. Yoshida places a small key on the shelf. An old key. A skeleton key. "The storage room. Behind the dead stock. There's a loose floorboard. Lift it. You'll find the door. I opened it once, in 1969. I've been too afraid to open it again."
Kai: "What's down there?"
Mr. Yoshida: "The truth. And my daughter."
He walks to the door. He pauses. "Don't tell your uncle. They're good people. They don't need to know."
The bell jingles. He's gone.
**Outside the window:** Yuki watches from the shadow of the awning. She sees her father leave. She sees Kai standing in the foreign section, holding the key. She presses her palm against the glass. Just for a moment. Then she's gone.

### Scene 3: "The War Council" (Episode 8, Act Three) — Yuki Returns
**Setting:** The store is closed. The blinds are drawn. The neon sign is off for the first time in years. The coven is gathered in the horror aisle. The Alpha Delta house has joined them. The store is packed — 15 vampires in a video store, all of them armed, all of them hungry.
**The atmosphere:** Tension. The air is thick. The vampires haven't fed in days. The elixir is gone. The Council cut the supply. The hunger is a living thing in the room. Rafa are at home, asleep. They don't know their store has been turned into a war room.
**The argument:**
Lucian (pacing): "This is insane. We're going to war with a council that's been running this campus for 150 years. With what army? A bunch of broke vampires from the art house?"
Rowan (quiet): "We have the tunnel. We have the tree. We have the element of surprise."
Lucian: "And what happens when we get to the tree? We tap it? We don't even know how."
Mara: "We learn."
Kai: "We have Yuki."
The room goes silent.
Kai: "Mr. Yoshida's daughter. She's bound to the tree. She's been a conduit for three years. She knows how it works. She knows how to tap it. And she knows how to burn it."
Mara: "She's also a volunteer. The Council doesn't control her. She's been waiting for a chance to destroy them."
Lucian: "And you trust her?"
Kai: "I trust that she wants the Council dead more than we do."
**Yuki enters.** The bell on the door jingles. Every vampire in the room turns. She's standing in the doorway — pale, thin, dark circles under her eyes. She's wearing a black coat over a Registrar's Office blouse. She looks like a ghost. She *is* a ghost. She's been dead to this world for three years.
**The moment:**
She walks to the foreign section. She touches the index cards in her own handwriting. She hasn't seen them in three years. She turns to face the coven.
"The tree is alive. It's been alive longer than this campus. It doesn't want to be fed. It wants to be *free.* The Council has been forcing it to feed on death for centuries. If we tap it, we don't just take the elixir. We *free* it. And when it's free, it will choose who to give its sap to. Not the Council. Not the Dean's Office. *Us.*"
Lucian: "And you know this how?"
Yuki: "Because I've been inside it. For three years. I've seen what it remembers. I've seen what it *wants.* And I've seen what happens if we don't do this."
She looks at Kai. "This store is the last place the Council will look. It's been under their nose the whole time. That's why it's the perfect place to start."
**The sound:** The neon sign flickers once, then holds. The coven starts to move. They're ready.

### Scene 4: "The Night Before" (Episode 10, Act One) — Yuki's Goodbye
**Setting:** The night before the assault on the tree. The store is closed. The coven is scattered around the store — some watching *The Craft* on the break room TV, some sleeping on the couch, some standing in the horror aisle, staring at the shelves. Nobody can sleep.
**The quiet moment:**
Kai is at the counter, alone. He's holding the amber vial from Episode 1. He's never opened it. He's been carrying it for weeks.
Mara walks up. She stands beside him. She doesn't look at him.
"You're not going to drink it."
Kai: "I don't know."
Mara: "You won't. It's not who you are."
Kai: "What if I don't know who I am anymore?"
Mara (quietly): "None of us do. That's why we have each other."
**Yuki's presence:** She's in the back office, alone. She's never been in this room before. She looks at the desk, the filing cabinet, the family photos. Rafa at a barbecue. Kai at 8, holding a stack of movies. A normal family. A normal life. The life she could have had.
She finds a post-it note and a pen. She writes: *"Thank you for keeping this store. You saved more lives than you know."* She sticks it on the monitor. She doesn't sign it. She doesn't need to.
**The beat:** Kai and Mara at the counter. Yuki in the back office. The store holds them all. It's the last night they'll all be together.
Kai: "What if we don't make it?"
Mara: "Then we make it count."
**The goodbye:** At 4 AM, Yuki walks to the front of the store. She looks at the foreign section one last time. She takes one of the index cards — the one for *The Last Wave* — and slips it into her pocket. She walks to the door. She turns to Kai, who's watching her from the counter.
"Tell your uncle the store is clean. I made sure of it."
Kai: "What do you mean?"
Yuki: "The floorboard. The cache. I emptied it. When the Council comes looking, there's nothing to find. Your uncle are safe. The store is clean."
She walks out. The bell jingles. The neon sign flickers. She's gone.

### Scene 5: "The Aftermath" (Episode 12, Act Three) — Yuki's Legacy
**Setting:** Three months later. The store is back to normal. The neon sign is on. The bell on the door jingles. Customers come and go. The world has moved on.
**The final scene:**
Kai is at the counter, organizing the new releases. *The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (2003) has just arrived on DVD. He's putting it in the display case. The coven is in the horror aisle, watching *The Craft* on the break room TV (again). They're laughing. They're alive.
Rafa is at his desk in the back office, drinking coffee. He notices the post-it note on the monitor. He's been staring at it for three months. He doesn't know who wrote it. He doesn't ask. Some things are better left unknown.
Rafa is at the counter, restocking the candy display. She looks at Kai. She looks at the coven. She smiles. "You've made friends."
Kai: "Yeah. I guess I have."
Linda: "They're weird."
Kai: "Yeah. They are."
Linda: "I like them."
**Yuki's legacy:** The foreign section is still there. The index cards in Yuki's handwriting are still on the shelf. Kai has added a fourth one. It's in his own handwriting, for a movie Yuki never got to see: *The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring.* The review reads: *"For Yuki. She would have loved this one."*
**The return:** At the end of the day, Kai is closing up. He checks the return slot. Inside, there's a single VHS tape. *The Last Wave* (1977). The one Yuki took the Staff Pick card for. The tape is rewound. On the case, a sticky note: *"I'm still here. — Y.Y."*
Kai's breath catches. He runs to the door. He looks out at the street. The neon sign flickers. The sidewalk is wet. The street is empty.
But at the end of the block, under a flickering streetlamp, a figure stands. A woman with dark hair, pale skin, wearing a black coat. She raises her hand. A wave. Then she turns and walks into the night.
**The final shot:**
A wide shot of the store. The neon sign in the window. The rows of tapes. The coven in the aisle. Kai at the counter, holding the tape. Rafa walks out of the back office, sees the look on his son's face.
Rafa: "What is it?"
Kai smiles. "Nothing. Just a returned tape."
He puts the tape on the "To Be Filed" cart. He doesn't tell anyone. Not yet. Some things are meant to be kept.
**The sound:** The doorbell. The neon hum. The faint sound of *The Craft* — "We are the weirdos, mister." The coven laughs. The credits roll. And somewhere, in the tunnels beneath the campus, a single root stirs. The tree is gone. But the tree is not gone.
