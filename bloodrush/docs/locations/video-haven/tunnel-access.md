# 4. THE HIDDEN TUNNEL ACCESS

### Location
The tunnel entrance is inside the **storage room** of Video Haven. The room is behind the back office, past a narrow hallway lined with boxes of inventory. The door is painted the same color as the wall (a faded beige), making it nearly invisible.
### The Entrance
The entrance is hidden **beneath a loose floorboard** in the storage room, behind the heavy shelving unit of dead stock. The floorboard is splintered along one edge — the only sign it's been lifted. Underneath: a trapdoor with an iron ring. The trapdoor is old, heavy, sealed with a padlock that looks like it's been there for a century.
**The padlock** is a Yale & Towne from 1890. It's rusted, antique, and keyed to a skeleton key. Kai finds the key in Episode 1 — it's tucked behind the loose floorboard, taped to the underside. He doesn't know what it opens. He keeps it.
### The Staircase
Beyond the trapdoor, a narrow staircase descends into the earth. The walls are unfinished stone, sweating with condensation. The stairs are steep, uneven, worn smooth by what looks like centuries of use. The air gets colder with every step. The hum gets louder.
There are **47 steps.** Kai counts them every time. The 47th step opens into the cave system.
### The Cave System
The tunnel connects to a **natural limestone cave network** beneath Veridian University. The caves are ancient — the university's founding documents mention them, but the Council sealed all public access in the 1920s.
The route from Video Haven to the tree chamber:
1. **Video Haven storage room** (47 steps down)
2. **The Junction** — a fork in the tunnel. Left leads to the Greek Row basements. Right leads to the tree chamber. Straight leads deeper, to a place Kai never explores.
3. **The Bone Gallery** — a narrow passage where the walls are embedded with human bones. The Council's dumping ground from the 1800s.
4. **The Anteroom** — a small chamber before the tree chamber. A stone altar. Candles. A single amber vial, always full, always waiting. The Council's offering station.
5. **The Tree Chamber** — the cavern. The tree. The roots. The bones.
### Who Knows
- **Kai's uncle Rafa** — don't know. The storage room is Rafa's least favorite part of the store. He never goes in there.
- **The Council** — knows the tunnel exists. They don't know the Santanas are oblivious. They're fine with it.
- **The Coven** — knows about the tunnel. Uses it when they need to. They don't know about the floorboard. They enter through the Greek Row entrance.
- **Mr. Yoshida** — knows about the tunnel. Found it in 1969. Has never told anyone. He's been waiting.
- **Kai** — doesn't know yet. He has the key. He's felt the draft. He's heard the hum. He'll find it in Episode 7.
