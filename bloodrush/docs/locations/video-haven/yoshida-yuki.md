# 3. THE BACKSTORY — Mr. Yoshida and Yuki

### Kenji Yoshida (born 1946)
Kenji Yoshida immigrated from Osaka in 1965. He was 19 years old. He had $200, a suitcase, and a letter of acceptance to Veridian University. He studied biology. He was the first person in his family to go to college.
In 1969, he was a graduate student studying the campus's unique cave system. He found the tree. He found the bones. He found the Council. They offered him a choice: forget what he saw, or join them. He refused both. He walked away. He never published his findings.
He got a job at the Registrar's Office. He married. He had a daughter. He lived a quiet life. He never stopped watching.
### Yuki Yoshida (born 1980)
Yuki grew up in Veridian. She was a curious child — always asking questions, always poking into things she shouldn't. Her father told her stories about the caves beneath the campus. She was fascinated. She was 17 when she found the entrance herself.
She didn't tell her father. She went down. She found the tree. She touched it. The tree chose her. The Council bound her.
She became a **conduit** — a living bridge between the tree and the Council's operations. Not a vampire. Not human. Something in between. Her body is alive, but her *self* is tethered to the tree. She can walk the campus. She works at the Registrar's Office, the same building as her father. She sees him in the hallway sometimes. She never speaks to him. She's not allowed to.
### The Connection to Video Haven
Mr. Yoshida started coming to Video Haven in 1986. He noticed the store's strange after-hours traffic. He noticed the hollowed tape cases. He noticed the amber vials.
He's been watching the store for 15 years. He knows it's a distribution hub. He knows Rafa doesn't know. He's been waiting for the right person to discover the tunnel. He's been waiting for Kai.
He's not a threat. He's a witness. And one day, he'll be an ally.
