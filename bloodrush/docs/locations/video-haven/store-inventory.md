# 2. THE STORE — Full 2003-Era Inventory

### Exterior
- **The sign:** A large rectangular neon sign mounted above the entrance — "Video Haven" in red cursive script, illuminated from within. The "V" flickers when the weather's humid. The "n" has been dim for three years. Rafa says he'll fix it next month. He's been saying that since 1998.
- **The windows:** Two large display windows flanking the glass door. Current display: a cardboard standee for *The Mummy Returns* (2003), a faded *The Matrix* poster (1999) behind it, and a hand-written sign: *"BE KIND, REWIND — $0.50 fee for unrewound tapes."*
- **The door:** A glass door with a brass handle. A decal on the glass reads "31" — the street number. Bells jingle when it opens. The sound is distinctive — a high-low chime. Kai hears it in his sleep.
- **The awning:** Striped red and white, faded to pink in places. It's about 12 feet off the ground. In the rain, it creates a dry pocket. The coven stands under it. They never enter together.
- **The sidewalk:** Concrete, cracked, stained with years of gum and rain. Wet at night, reflecting the neon glow. A single cigarette butt always in the same spot — Malachi's brand.
### Interior Layout
#### The Front Counter (Immediate left upon entry)
- **The register:** A 1990s NCR cash register with manual keys. It makes a satisfying *cha-ching* sound. Rafa refuses to upgrade to a computer system. "The computer doesn't know Mr. Henderson likes his rentals on Tuesdays."
- **The countertop:** Dark wood, worn smooth by decades of elbows. A plexiglass display case holds: Gum (Juicy Fruit, Big Red, Bubble Yum, $0.50), Now & Laters, Lemonheads, Hot Tamales, Mike and Ike's, a jar of pickled eggs (Rafa's, not for sale), a small bowl of free mints.
- **Behind the counter:** A wall of rental cards in wooden slots, organized alphabetically by last name. A landline phone with a spiral cord. A 13-inch CRT TV with a built-in VCR — plays the store's security feed (grainy black-and-white, four cameras, the tape rewinds every 24 hours). A mug of pens. A framed photo of Kai at 8 years old, holding a stack of movies.
- **The bulletin board:** Stapled with flyers for lost cats, a campus production of *The Crucible*, a "Tutor Wanted" card, and a coupon for a local pizza place.

#### The Main Aisle (VHS — The Heart of the Store)

The store smells like: **old plastic, dust, carpet cleaner, paperboard, and the faint chemical sweetness of rewinding VHS tapes.**

#### The Storage Room
- A narrow room past the back office. Boxes of inventory. Extra VHS cases. Promotional materials. A shelf of "dead stock" — returned tapes that are too damaged to rent, boxes of empty VHS cases, old promotional materials, and a decade of *Entertainment Weekly* magazines.
- **The drop:** Behind the dead-stock shelf, there's a loose floorboard. Underneath: a small cache of amber vials, wrapped in a cloth. Kai found it in Episode 1. He didn't tell anyone. He's been watching it. The vials come and go. He doesn't know who restocks them. He doesn't know who picks them up. But he's seen the coven's eyes linger on the storage room door.
- **The tunnel:** The floorboard also hides a trapdoor. Iron ring. Stone stairs below. Kai doesn't know this yet. He's never lifted the board. He's felt the draft. He's heard the hum. He's told himself it's the pipes.
### The Elixir — How the Distribution Works
The amber vials are a **synthetic blood substitute** — the elixir — that suppresses vampire hunger. The Council controls the supply. The vials are distributed through a network of "neutral ground" locations across campus. Video Haven is one of them.
**The system:**
1. A courier (Malik) drops a fresh supply of vials into the storage room cache every Sunday night, after closing.
2. Coven members and other affiliated vampires pick up their weekly supply by leaving an empty vial in a designated drop box (the hollowed *Lost Boys* case or the foreign section empty cases) and collecting a full one from the cache.
3. The exchange happens during regular business hours, hidden in plain sight. A vampire rents a movie. They browse the foreign section. They swap the vials. They leave.
4. **Kai's uncle Rafa have no idea.** Rafa found a loose vial once. He thought it was a weird lip gloss. Rafa threw it away.
5. **Kai starts noticing in Episode 1.** He finds the first vial in the *Lost Boys* case. He starts watching the pattern. He starts collecting the empties. He doesn't know what he's holding. But he knows it's important.
### The Regulars
- **The 11 PM Crowd:** College students, mostly. They come in after the library closes. They browse for 45 minutes. They rent one movie. They never return it on time.
- **The Night Manager:** Kai. He works the closing shift, 9 PM to midnight. He cleans the tapes, organizes the returns, rewinds everything. He's the only employee his parents trust to close alone.
- **The Coven:** They don't rent. They *lurk.* They stand in the horror aisle, talking in code, watching the door. They pick up their vials and leave. They never buy anything. They never leave empty-handed. Rafa thinks they're "those goth kids from the art department."
- **Mr. Yoshida:** A regular. Japanese, elderly, quiet. He comes in every Tuesday and Friday at 7 PM. He always rents one foreign film. He always pays with exact change. He never speaks unless spoken to. He's been coming to the store for 15 years. He knows about the vials. He knows about the tunnel. He's been watching. He's been waiting.
- **Rafa Santana:** The owner. He's behind the counter from 10 AM to 10 PM, every day except Sunday. He sits on a stool, reads the newspaper, drinks coffee, and watches the store through the security feed. He's seen everything. He's noticed nothing.
- **Rafa Santana:** The heart. She's at the counter during the day, chatting with customers, restocking candy, making coffee. She's the reason the store still has regulars. She's the reason Kai still shows up.
