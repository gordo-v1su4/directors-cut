# Blood Rush — Character Bible

## Kai “Hoodie” Santana — 19

Main lead. A former high-school football star who arrived at college without joining the team. He is athletic, magnetic, cocky, and fiercely protective. Kai is not book-smart and knows it; he survives through street intelligence, social reads, nerve, and the ability to make a lie feel like a favor.

Kai is gay and closeted. This is not expressed through flamboyant behavior, costume shorthand, vocal affectation, or stereotype. In fall 2003, inside a Catholic-college environment and a family system already strained by football, money, and expectation, he treats his sexuality as one more truth that could cost him belonging. His easy charm is real and reaches across genders, but he is practiced at letting other people make the safest assumption about him.

- Public mask: relaxed campus celebrity who always knows where the party is and never appears cornered by a personal question
- Inner wound: fears that without football, charm, or the crew, there is nothing underneath; fears that being fully known would make family and crew loyalty conditional
- Private truth: gay, closeted, and more frightened of needing love openly than of surviving danger
- Power: controls access, information, and the suppressant supply more effectively than formal authority
- Humor: confident improvisation, playful deflection, laughing when a performance becomes too earnest
- Audition test: charm turning into command without losing likability

## Mara Voss — 19

Audience-entry co-lead. A vigilant, introspective freshman who begins the pilot human. She notices what other people dismiss, but her instinct is to contain fear rather than announce it. Her unauthorized turning makes her both vulnerable and politically dangerous.

- Public mask: self-contained, prepared, difficult to embarrass
- Inner wound: believes needing help gives other people ownership over her
- Power: a rare bloodline anomaly expressed first through black blood-tears
- Humor: dry observation and disbelief, especially when supernatural rules sound absurd
- Audition test: fear, intelligence, and control without melodrama

## Elias Mercer — 20

Rowan's older brother and a newly turned vampire. Elias lost a year and now shares freshman status with his younger brother. He is watchful, volatile, and magnetic, with shame hiding beneath protective authority.

- Public mask: older brother who has everything handled
- Inner wound: feels time has exposed him as the brother who fell behind
- Relationship: loves Rowan, resents Rowan's competence, and confuses protection with control
- Humor: defensive irritation when Rowan is objectively correct
- Audition test: authority cracking into fear without becoming a tantrum

## Rowan Mercer — 19

Elias's younger brother. Reserved, perceptive, and quietly defiant. Rowan is more academically capable and less socially dominant, which makes sharing freshman year with Elias both protective and humiliating.

- Public mask: detached observer who does not care about the hierarchy
- Inner wound: fears that loyalty to Elias will consume his own life
- Relationship: sees through Elias but cannot stop protecting him
- Humor: precise corrections, delayed smirks, and badly timed honesty
- Audition test: active listening, restrained resentment, and a choice to stay

## Lucian Vale — 19

A newly changed freshman who is watchful, volatile, and fiercely loyal once trust is earned. Lucian recognizes Mara's terror before he understands her anomaly. Their attraction is based on recognition, not destiny, and remains secondary to the mystery.

- Public mask: cool distance and practiced indifference
- Inner wound: believes closeness makes him dangerous to the other person
- Relationship to Mara: restrained recognition and unwanted tenderness
- Humor: clipped honesty that lands drier than intended
- Audition test: intimacy and danger held in the same quiet performance

## Malachi Jackson — 20

A street-born vampire enforcer. Malachi is controlled, dangerous, and watchful. He does not need volume to dominate a room and is most frightening when he sounds reasonable.

- Public mask: calm student who rarely participates
- Inner wound: measures his worth by usefulness and fears becoming disposable
- Power: disciplined physical control and loyalty under pressure
- Humor: deadpan literalism and a refusal to indulge dramatic people
- Audition test: threat, restraint, and a flicker of private concern

## Malik Vale — 21

The crew's night runner: guarded, fearless, lean, and built for movement. Malik transports messages, suppressant, and people when normal channels are unsafe. He looks socially fearless but is always calculating an exit.

- Public mask: untouchable confidence
- Inner wound: expects every group to use him and leave him exposed
- Power: speed, nerve, and the ability to sell a cover story under pressure
- Humor: audacious lies followed by an involuntary grin
- Audition test: improvisation shifting into genuine fear

## Kenji Yoshida — 57

Registrar's Office clerk. Immigrated from Osaka in 1965; found the campus tree and Council in 1969; refused both silence and service. A quiet witness who has watched Video Haven's after-hours traffic for decades. Not a fighter — a father waiting to help the right person find the tunnel.

## Yuki Yoshida — 23

Bound to the amber source tree as a **conduit** — not vampire, not fully human. Works the Registrar's Office; cannot speak to her father. Slips cryptic staff-pick cards and notes through Video Haven's foreign section. Her sacrifice arc is the season's moral hinge.

## The Council (Dean's Office)

**Dean Vane**, **Cross**, and **Finch** — ancient vampires administering Veridian like a cartel. They control suppressant supply, Greek-row recruitment, and Yuki's binding. Background pressure in early episodes; open antagonists by mid-season.

## Core relationship map

- Kai ↔ Mara: strategic protector and resistant new liability; intimate lead chemistry rooted in recognition and trust, never a heterosexual romance
- Mara ↔ Lucian: quiet recognition and restrained attraction
- Elias ↔ Rowan: brothers caught between love, shame, authority, and resentment
- Kai ↔ Elias: competing versions of leadership—social control versus familial control
- Kai ↔ Malachi: trust built on usefulness, with an unspoken fear of disobedience
- Kai ↔ Malik: mutual appreciation for improvisation, limited trust around the suppressant supply
