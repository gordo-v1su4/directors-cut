# Hype Slate — 40 Trailer Concepts

Working slate of original film, series, commercial, and live-event ideas built for teaser-trailer heat.

Not IP pitches of existing franchises. Each concept is designed so a 15–30s Seedance/Sora teaser could make someone say: *I need that.*

## Why these 40

Concepts are weighted toward what is currently **making money**, **holding streaming attention**, and **driving search** (mid-2025 → July 2026):

| Signal | What audiences are rewarding |
| --- | --- |
| Box office | Animation/myth spectacle, music culture films, sports prestige, liminal horror, sci-fi survival, obsession thrillers |
| Streaming | Mystery thrillers, true-crime adjacent docs, K-action, fantasy adventure, reality heat, comfort nostalgia |
| Demand analytics | Anime/action-fantasy/horror/sci-fi undersupplied vs demand; crime franchises travel; reality volume ≠ breakout |
| Search / culture | Summerween slashers, concert tours + nostalgia duos, live music spectacle > ads, female-vocalist pop heat |

Full research notes: [`MARKET-SIGNALS.md`](MARKET-SIGNALS.md)

## Mix (40)

- **18 films** — theatrical / event-movie energy
- **12 series** — binge / weekly appointment heat
- **5 commercials** — brand spots that feel like culture, not ads
- **5 live / DJ-concert events** — trailerable spectacle nights

## How to cut

1. Skim [`concepts/00-INDEX.md`](concepts/00-INDEX.md)
2. Mark Keep / Maybe / Kill in [`SHORTLIST.md`](SHORTLIST.md)
3. Target a final board of **8–12** before writing full trailer prompts

## Trailer test (use when shortlisting)

Ask for each concept:

1. Can the first 3 seconds show a world people already want?
2. Is there one image that would stop a scroll?
3. Does the title slam feel inevitable after the last beat?
