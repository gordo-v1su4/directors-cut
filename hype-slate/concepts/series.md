# Series (S01–S12)

Each card: logline · short description · teaser hook · why now.

---

## S01 — COLD OPEN

**Format:** 8-ep mystery thriller  
**Tone:** Agonized, propulsive, twisty

**Logline:** A father released after ten years for his child’s murder gets one anonymous tip — the child is alive — and every clue he follows rewrites the night of the crime.

**Short description:**
- Appointment-TV engine: each episode invalidates the last episode’s “truth.”
- Dual timelines: prison memory vs. present hunt.
- Ensemble of suspects who all have a version that almost works.

**Teaser hook:** “They said I killed my son.” — doorbell. A child-sized silhouette. “Dad?”

**Why now:** *I Will Find You* sitting #1 globally shows this exact shape is what people are finishing right now.

---

## S02 — NEXT DOOR

**Format:** 6-ep true-crime anthology  
**Tone:** Neighborly dread, documentary hybrid

**Logline:** A charming true-crime host investigates “worst neighbors” across America — until season one’s final case is filmed from inside his own HOA.

**Short description:**
- Body-cam, Ring footage, bake-sale politeness as horror.
- Anthology cases that feed a season-long identity twist.
- The host’s smile is the brand and the tell.

**Teaser hook:** “Tonight’s neighbor… is me.” — porch light flickers; camera turns around.

**Why now:** *Worst Neighbor Ever* / true-crime spinoffs keep charting; meta-host angle makes it trailerable and renewably franchisable.

---

## S03 — GHOST PROTOCOL: SEOUL

**Format:** 10-ep K-action drama  
**Tone:** Neon, ruthless, stylish

**Logline:** A retired black-ops agent wakes up to find her face on every public wanted screen in Seoul — for crimes she will commit over the next 72 hours.

**Short description:**
- Subway chases, rooftop gun-kata, chaebol conspiracy.
- Future-crime frame without copying named IP: the city’s CCTV predicts her.
- Partner is the detective hunting her — also her ex.

**Teaser hook:** LED billboard: her face. Caption: WANTED — TOMORROW.

**Why now:** *Agent Kim Reactivated* style K-action is topping Netflix world; global demand for Korean series remains a growth engine.

---

## S04 — CROWNFIRE

**Format:** Prestige fantasy · 8-ep season  
**Tone:** Operatic, political, scorched

**Logline:** When the queen dies, the throne is claimed by whoever can wear the living crown of wildfire — and three heirs would rather burn the realm than kneel.

**Short description:**
- HotD search energy: succession, dragons-as-system replaced by crownfire bonds.
- Court intrigue + siege spectacle.
- Clear house iconography for fan casting / merch.

**Teaser hook:** Crown lowered onto a brow. Hair ignites. The court applauds anyway.

**Why now:** House of the Dragon is a top summer search; prestige fantasy still drives appointment viewing and fandom intensity.

## S05 — THE VILLA

**Format:** Reality-thriller hybrid · 10 eps  
**Tone:** Glossy, vicious, addictive

**Logline:** Ten singles enter a luxury villa for love and votes — unaware the producers have already decided two of them won’t leave alive, and the audience is helping choose who.

**Short description:**
- Love Island DNA weaponized into thriller.
- Confessionals that contradict what we just saw.
- “America votes” becomes a moral crime.

**Teaser hook:** Text on screen: SEND HOME. Cut to body bag. Same font.

**Why now:** Love Island USA is the #1 summer TV search; format recognition + murder stakes = instant teaser literacy.

## S06 — LEVEL ZERO

**Format:** Action fantasy · 8–10 eps  
**Tone:** Power fantasy, street-level myth

**Logline:** Under a megacity, a black-market dungeon economy lets civilians buy monster cores — until a broke courier absorbs a core that levels him without a license, making him illegal to exist.

**Short description:**
- Solo Leveling / gate-fantasy energy as original: ranks, raids, neon undercity.
- System UI as diegetic holograms (trailer candy).
- Weekly boss escalation; season villain is the licensing board.

**Teaser hook:** Phone notification: LEVEL UP — then SWAT kicks the door.

**Why now:** Anime/action-fantasy overperform supply; live-action gate fantasies are the franchise template Parrot Analytics keeps flagging.

---

## S07 — PRAIRIE SIGNAL

**Format:** Period + sci-fi hybrid · 8 eps  
**Tone:** Warm first, then uncanny

**Logline:** A 1880s frontier town begins receiving radio broadcasts from 2026 — weather alerts, pop songs, and missing-person reports that name people still alive in the village.

**Short description:**
- Little House comfort aesthetic invaded by future signal.
- Family drama carries; sci-fi mystery pulls.
- Perfect contrast teaser: butter churn / EDM drop / dust storm.

**Teaser hook:** “This is an emergency alert for—” — cut to a prairie church that shouldn’t know those words.

**Why now:** *Little House* nostalgia is charting hard; sci-fi is undersupplied; hybrid is the gap.

---

## S08 — THE APARTMENT BELOW

**Format:** Crime heist procedural · 8 eps  
**Tone:** Clever, claustrophobic, funny-dark

**Logline:** A crew plans one perfect building heist — but each episode they can only clear one floor, and every floor’s residents are running a crime of their own.

**Short description:**
- Vertical anthology inside a single plan.
- Elevator as chapter card.
- Finale is the basement that shouldn’t exist.

**Teaser hook:** “We go floor by floor.” — security cam montage of eight different felonies already in progress.

**Why now:** Crime drama franchises travel globally; contained high-concept is cheap to teaser and rich to season.

## S09 — BLOOD MARKET

**Format:** Medical thriller · 8 eps  
**Tone:** Clinical glam, moral rot

**Logline:** At invitation-only auctions, the ultra-rich buy “spare lives” grown from stolen DNA — and a ER nurse recognizes her missing sister’s face on the catalog.

**Short description:**
- Black-tie medical horror.
- Each episode a lot / a buyer / a leak.
- Romance subplot with a buyer who might be trying to burn it down.

**Teaser hook:** Gavel. “Lot 17: 28 years, unused.” — sister’s eyes open in a tank.

**Why now:** Thriller + drama dominate streaming prefs; body/immortality anxiety is evergreen and visually shocking.

## S10 — WIDOW’S FREQUENCY

**Format:** Coastal mystery · 6–8 eps  
**Tone:** Melancholy, addictive puzzle

**Logline:** In a fishing town, every woman who has ever lost a husband hears the same song on the radio the night before another man dies — and this season the song starts early.

**Short description:**
- Small-town ensemble; folkloric rules; detective outsider.
- Music as clue system (needle drops = plot).
- Season mystery with anthology deaths.

**Teaser hook:** Static. A love song. Three widows look up at the same time.

**Why now:** Coastal/mystery “Widow’s Bay”-style search heat + music-forward storytelling matches summer listen culture.

---

## S11 — DIVISION 9

**Format:** Superhero-adjacent series · 10 eps  
**Tone:** Gritty civic myth

**Logline:** When a city’s power grid starts granting abilities to whoever is touching a live wire during blackouts, a utility worker becomes the only person who can turn heroes off.

**Short description:**
- Capes without costumes: hard hats, transformers, rain.
- Demand for superhero series without MCU fatigue aesthetics.
- Weekly powered threat; season arc is privatization of the grid.

**Teaser hook:** Blackout. Spark. A kid floats. The lineman flips a switch — and drops him.

**Why now:** Superhero series remain high-demand; ground-level civic angle feels fresh and trailerable.

---

## S12 — REPLAY

**Format:** Sports crime · 8 eps  
**Tone:** Stylish, paranoid, athletic

**Logline:** Elite athletes discover their games are being edited in advance — highlight packages leak before kickoff — and the league’s VR “practice sims” are writing the real outcomes.

**Short description:**
- Locker rooms, betting apps, deepfake crowd noise.
- Star athlete + analyst conspiracy duo.
- Each episode a different sport / same editor in the sky.

**Teaser hook:** Pregame hype video shows a touchdown that hasn’t happened — then it does, frame for frame.

**Why now:** Sports prestige + streaming crime; live sports already dominate search spikes — this fictionalizes the paranoia around scripts and betting.
