# Films (F01–F18)

Each card: logline · short description · teaser hook · why now.

## F01 — NEPHILIM

**Format:** Theatrical myth spectacle · ~2h  
**Tone:** Epic, operatic, brutal beauty

**Logline:** A river orphan who was never meant to be born steals a god’s name — and heaven sends collectors who look like family.

**Short description:**
- Mythic China-adjacent world of floating tribunals, red-thread contracts, and gods who outsource punishment.
- Protagonist fights with borrowed divinity that burns them alive a little more each use.
- Final image: the orphan’s silhouette walking into a sky that has started to crack like porcelain.

**Teaser hook:** “They gave me a name so they could erase it.” — title slam over a child catching lightning in a clay bowl.

**Why now:** Ne Zha 2 proved myth spectacle is the highest-grossing theatrical bet on Earth; audiences want original god-stories that feel franchise-sized in one glance.

**Tone:** Precision, sweat, paranoia

**Logline:** A legendary driver’s final season becomes a heist when the pit wall realizes someone is rewriting race telemetry in real time — and betting the deaths.

**Short description:**

**Teaser hook:** “We don’t lose races. We get edited out of them.”

**Why now:** F1: The Movie (~$634M) proved sports prestige + velocity is a theatrical event; sabotage thriller sharpens the watchability.
