# Live / DJ-Concert Events (L01–L05)

These are event IPs you could teaser like a movie — ticket drops, aftermovies, and festival films.

---

## L01 — AFTERDARK CIRCUIT

**Format:** One-night city festival · trailer as “event film”  
**Tone:** Illegal-feeling, glamorous, relentless

**Logline:** One wristband. Seven secret venues. From rooftop golden hour to a sealed underground final — the city becomes a single DJ set that never stops moving.

**Short description:**
- Teaser structure: map pins lighting up, crowd running between districts, bass bleeding through subway grates.
- Headliner identity locked late (mystery drop fuel).
- Aftermovie is the product; tickets are the CTA.

**Teaser hook:** “If you hear it, you’re already late.” — skyline pulse-synced to kick drum.

**Why now:** Concert tour searches dominate music intent; scavenger-style nights create FOMO content that outruns static festival ads.

---

## L02 — DUO RESET

**Format:** Stadium nostalgia dual-headline tour  
**Tone:** Reunion lightning, generational scream

**Logline:** Two artists who defined an era share one stage for the first time in 15 years — and the show is structured as a “reset”: their old hits collide into new collabs in real time.

**Short description:**
- Rides Google’s nostalgia duo-tour search spike (Chris Brown/Usher, Ne-Yo/Akon pattern) as original event framing.
- Trailer: split-screen young vs now, then the hug / the drop.
- Merch and live album built into the night’s narrative.

**Teaser hook:** Two silhouettes. Caption: LAST TIME. Cut: FIRST TIME AGAIN.

**Why now:** Nostalgia duo tours are among 2026’s top trending concert searches; the teaser sells unfinished business.

---

## L03 — MAIN STAGE BLACKOUT

**Format:** Festival tentpole night / cinematic aftermovie  
**Tone:** Apocalyptic euphoria

**Logline:** Mid-set, the main stage loses all power — and the headliner finishes the show from a single battery deck in the mud while 80,000 phones become the light show.

**Short description:**
- Disaster as design: no LED walls, only human constellation.
- Trailer sells the myth of the night before tickets drop for next year.
- DJ-as-survivor iconography.

**Teaser hook:** Screens die. Crowd screams. One kick drum in the dark. Phones rise like a galaxy.

**Why now:** Live music moments beat ads on engagement; “I was there when the lights died” is pure share fuel.

---

## L04 — FLOATING 160

**Format:** Harbor boat-to-boat BPM war  
**Tone:** Dawn rave, competitive joy

**Logline:** At 4am, twenty boats dock into a floating grid. DJs battle at 160 BPM until sunrise — last boat still dancing wins the harbor crown.

**Short description:**
- Visuals money can’t fake easily: fog, wakes, laser between masts, sunrise drop.
- Perfect for short-form teaser + long aftermovie.
- Brand partners as “fleet sponsors,” not stage banners.

**Teaser hook:** Anchor chains rattle like a countdown. First drop hits as a ferry horn answers.

**Why now:** Unique geography = scroll-stopping; festival fatigue needs new arenas; dawn sets match “song of summer” culture.

---

## L05 — GODMODE SET

**Format:** Esports arena × DJ spectacle  
**Tone:** Competitive, cyber-glam, loud

**Logline:** A live DJ set becomes a ranked match: the crowd’s motion, chat spam, and player K/D steer the drop — miss the cue and the arena “wipes.”

**Short description:**
- Gaming + concert fusion for Gen Z spend.
- Trailer: crowd as boss fight, VFX synced to HP bars, DJ as final raid DPS.
- Seasonable: weekly ranked nights, championship weekend.

**Teaser hook:** “Crowd HP: 3%.” — drop. “SERVER SAVED.”

**Why now:** Game IP owns box office; esports already eventizes attention; music+games is the underbuilt live format.
