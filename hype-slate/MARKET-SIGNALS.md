# Market Signals (as of July 2026)

Source mix: Box Office Mojo worldwide charts, Netflix/FlixPatrol Top 10, Google Summergeist 2026, Parrot Analytics demand reports, Super Bowl LX culture analysis.

## Box office — what prints money

**2025 worldwide leaders (pattern, not titles to copy):**

- Mythic / animated spectacle at massive scale (Ne Zha 2 ~$2.27B, Zootopia 2 ~$1.87B)
- Family / game / toy IP that feels like an event (Mario, Minecraft, Lilo & Stitch, How to Train Your Dragon)
- Spectacle sci-fi (Avatar: Fire and Ash ~$1.49B)
- Anime theatrical as mainstream (Demon Slayer Infinity Castle ~$0.7–0.8B)
- Sports prestige drama (F1: The Movie ~$634M)
- Superhero still viable but no longer automatic (#10–13 range)

**2026 YTD leaders reinforcing the same bets:**

- Game/animation event films (Super Mario Galaxy Movie ~$1.01B, Toy Story 5 ~$0.88B)
- Music icon / culture film (Michael ~$1.00B)
- Fashion / workplace obsession sequel heat (Devil Wears Prada 2 ~$688M)
- Hard sci-fi survival (Project Hail Mary ~$684M)
- Liminal / internet-horror going theatrical (Backrooms ~$364M)
- Obsession / erotic thriller breakout (Obsession ~$427M + top trending trailer searches)

**Takeaway for teasers:** big visual worlds, clear hero silhouettes, music/culture identity, sports velocity, and “something wrong with this place” horror all convert.

## Streaming — what people actually finish

Netflix world Top TV (mid-July 2026 snapshot): mystery-thriller appointment (*I Will Find You*), comfort nostalgia (*Little House*), K-action (*Agent Kim Reactivated*), fantasy adventure (*Avatar: The Last Airbender*), true-crime neighbor horror (*Worst Neighbor Ever*).

Netflix genre preference (Q2 2026 FlixPatrol): Drama + Comedy lead, then Thriller, Action, Animated, Crime, Documentary, Horror. Sci-fi/fantasy share looks small in “preference %” but demand analytics say those genres are **undersupplied relative to appetite**.

Google Summergeist summer TV search: Love Island USA, House of the Dragon, Little House — reality heat + prestige fantasy + comfort.

**Takeaway for teasers:** cold-open mystery, “someone is watching you” true crime, glossy K-action, and fantasy geography all hype cleanly in 15–30s.

## Demand analytics — supply gaps worth filling

Parrot Analytics (2025 → 2026):

- High-demand / strong travel: anime, action fantasy, superhero series, crime drama franchises
- Undersupplied vs appetite (especially US streamers): science fiction, horror, animation, fantasy
- Oversupplied for breakouts: talk, lifestyle reality, broad romance — steady but rarely travel
- Global engines: Korean series + anime IP pipelines (including live-action adaptations)

**Takeaway for teasers:** originality wins when it *looks* franchisable in one glance — power systems, creature rules, hunt logic, arena rules.

## Culture / search / live

- “Summerween” + slasher searches doubled (Google Summergeist)
- Concert tours and nostalgia duo tours dominate music search
- Super Bowl LX: live music / culture moments crushed traditional ads on engagement; celebrity + vibe spots (Charli xcx / Poppi style) and nostalgia concert stunts still cut through
- Female vocalists dominate song-of-summer search heat
- Sports live events still spike search hard during play

**Takeaway for teasers:** DJ/concert concepts should feel like a night people would fly for; commercials should feel like a music video or surprise concert, not a product demo.
