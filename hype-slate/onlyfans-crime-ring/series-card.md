# ONLYFANS CRIME RING

## Logline

A broke Gen Z creator joins an “OnlyFans house” that promises safety and splits — then discovers the real product isn’t content, it’s identity packs, blackmail archives, crypto mules, and emotional manipulation sold as intimacy.

## Tagline

The subscription is the trap.

## Pertains to brief

- **Gen Z survival thriller:** rent, visas, tuition, phones, platform bans — crime as cashflow
- **Vice injection:** intimacy economy backstage — ring lights, chats, mods, agencies, soft mansions
- **Euphoria × scam culture:** neon nightlife + betrayal, but the engine is **data and leverage**, not sex spectacle
- **Shonda:** you think predatory manager story → the house is a cybercrime studio wearing lingerie
- **Breaking Bad:** the “ops girl” who organizes chats/schedules becomes the architect of the blackmail OS

## What this is NOT

- Softcore / porn-forward prestige that sells the body as the show
- A morality lecture about sex work
- Generic “catfish” of the week procedural
- Straight cartel story with creators as decoration

## What this IS

Cybercrime + intimacy economy.  
The hook is **data, manipulation, blackmail, crypto, and identity construction**.  
Sex is the storefront. The warehouse is inboxes, deepfakes, stolen KYC, and subscribers who can be ruined.

## Pilot engine

**She is not a criminal yet.**

**Maya** joins a content house for protection after a solo account gets drained by chargebacks and a stalker.  
House rules feel like family: chores, content calendars, security, a “momager.”  
Pilot mystery: creators who “graduate” leave their phones behind — and their faces keep earning.  
Maya’s skill is emotional labor in DMs; she builds a better CRM than the house has ever had.  
Pilot end: she finds the archive — not nudes, **leverage files** on whales — and her login is already admin.

## Characters

- **Maya Reyes** — new creator; DM prodigy; audience empathy as superpower / curse
- **Sasha** — house “momager”; brand soft voice; runs the OS
- **Jules** — top earner; golden cage; knows and can’t leave
- **Theo** — crypto / payments guy; “just the money rails”; actually the mule network
- **Noah** — whale subscriber who thinks he’s in love; becomes leverage or liability

## World

Sunbelt creator mansion (Miami or LA-adjacent) + nightlife that funds the myth of freedom.  
Visuals: ring lights, acrylic nails on mechanical keyboards, group chats, fake passports in makeup bags, crypto seed phrases in notes apps, club bathrooms as meeting rooms, apartment glass that looks like success and feels like a tank.  
Subculture: agencies, discord mods, account farms, “face doubles,” AI likeness clauses nobody reads.

## Shonda hook

Maya’s first big collab night: she hits a payout that could change her family’s month.  
Celebration in the club.  
Floor drop: the money isn’t from her page — it’s a whale paying to suppress a leak. The leak is a deepfake of Maya they already made… as a demo of what happens if she leaves.

## Breaking Bad pivot

Maya doesn’t become a villain for thrills. She becomes the person who can **run retention** — scripts, schedules, emotional cadence — so well that the house makes her Head of Intimacy Ops.  
She tries to protect the girls by professionalizing the blackmail… and invents a cleaner crime.

## Season engine

Escalation ladder:

1. Account takeover / identity packs  
2. Whale blackmail as “customer success”  
3. Cross-border crypto mule chains  
4. Rival houses / platform trust-and-safety war  
5. A creator death that must be framed as “offline for mental health”  

Episode rule: every episode a relationship feels real — then reveals which side was productized.

## Teaser rules

**Part 1 may show:** mansion soft life, ring lights, DM heart emojis, nightlife, burner phones, sisterhood, one glitch (wrong face on a stream / payout that doesn’t match).

**Part 2 may show:** archive room energy, crypto transfers, identity collage walls, betrayal, a whale ruined, Maya realizing she’s already inside the machine — danger as screens and silence more than bullets.
