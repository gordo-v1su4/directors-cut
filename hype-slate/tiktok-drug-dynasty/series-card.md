# TIKTOK DRUG DYNASTY

## Logline

Two Miami girls accidentally invent the first viral narcotic designed for the attention economy — sold as a beauty / sleep / focus / mood wellness drop — and by the time they understand what they made, obsession has already scaled faster than crime.

## Tagline

Fame is the new cartel.

## Pertains to brief

- **Breaking Bad:** ordinary competence (content + kitchen chemistry) → product that shouldn’t matter → life rewritten by demand; moral math worsens as success works
- **Shonda:** you think soft-launch girlboss glow-up → it’s the birth of a social contagion
- **Vice / subculture:** injected into TikTok beauty + Miami nightlife micro-worlds; glam and rot in the same frame
- **Gen Z:** livestream wellness, overnight shipping, analytics addiction, soft launches — Netflix season length, TikTok-native surface
- **Not vampires / not Bay-first:** seductive accidental empire; violence arrives late

## What this is NOT

- Drug dealers invent meth
- Guns/cartels from the cold open
- A PSA about fentanyl
- Generic “influencer does crime” with no product invention

## What this IS

Accidental genius inside a beauty startup aesthetic.  
An unregulated analog they can **legally sell at first**.  
Virality is the high. Addiction is the reveal. Fame becomes the cartel.

## Pilot engine

They are **not criminals yet**.

They think they’re building:

- a beauty brand
- a sleep aid
- a focus supplement
- a mood enhancer
- a livestream wellness product

Then people become obsessed.  
Legal gray zone keeps the door open long enough for the brand to become unavoidable.  
Pilot ends on secrecy-as-business-plan: *“We can’t tell anybody what this is.”*

## Tone

## Characters

- **Nina** — ops/analytics brain; the dashboard is her moral event
- **Best friend / co-founder** — first taste; seals the pact with the secrecy line
- *(Season support, not teaser-required):* early believers, club gatekeepers, a regulator/journalist shadow, the first casualty who still looks like a customer

## World

Miami. Pink-LED apartment lab that reads as TikTok skincare startup. Ring lights, shipping boxes, overnight labels, cyan clubs, analytics screens, soft-launch packaging that becomes ritual object.

## Shonda hook

Episode 1 plays like a chaotic success story — comments, Venmo, follower spikes, “this actually works.”  
Floor drop: the secrecy isn’t shame. It’s strategy. They’ve crossed from product to conspiracy without a crime scene.

## Breaking Bad pivot

Not “they decide to deal.”  
Nina’s competence at scaling (SKU logic, shipping, content loops) makes her the empire’s nervous system — while she still tells herself it’s a brand. Crime identity arrives as consequence of optimization.

## Season engine

Each batch / drop / platform wave solves a problem and creates a worse one: stockouts → cutting → copycats → attention heat → institutional eyes → nightlife swallowing them → no clean exit that doesn’t kill the brand or the users.

## Teaser rules

**Part 1 — THE DISCOVERY must show:** discovery, startup energy, first taste, virality, temptation, money climbing, secrecy beat.  
**Part 1 must NOT show:** guns, cartels, raids, teeth-in-sink horror.

**Part 2 — THE VIRUS may show:** obsession, warehouse scale, club ritual, cult-celebrity fear, addiction beginning, first violent/raid flashes only at the peak.  
End on: *What did we make?* / **FAME IS THE NEW CARTEL.**

## Trailer campaign (outline only)

1. **Part 1 — THE DISCOVERY** (15s) — mysterious, sexy, exciting, accidental genius → audience thinks “they made the next cocaine”
