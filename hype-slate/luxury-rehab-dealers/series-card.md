# LUXURY REHAB DEALERS

## Logline

A rising sober-living director at an oceanfront Malibu–Miami recovery empire discovers the clinic’s ketamine therapy, celebrity aftercare, and “wellness logistics” are the cleanest cartel distribution and laundering system on either coast — and her gift for keeping patients alive makes her too valuable to leave.

## Tagline

Healing is the cover.

## Pertains to brief

- **Vice / high society:** injected into elite recovery culture — celebrities, private chefs, oceanfront mansions, sober influencers, ketamine lounges
- **Breaking Bad:** competence in caretaking becomes criminal infrastructure; she optimizes “healing” until she can’t tell which part is medicine
- **Shonda:** you think prestige wellness exposé → the miracle protocol *is* the pipeline
- **Gen Z surface:** sober-Tok, IV drip soft-launches, retreat content, biohacking language as camouflage
- **Tonally fresh:** weaponizes healing culture instead of street corners

## What this is NOT

- Street-level cartel soap with guns in alleyways from minute one
- A joke about “rich people in rehab”
- Celebrity gossip with drugs sprinkled on
- TikTok Drug Dynasty 2 (different engine: institution + laundering, not inventing a viral product)

## What this IS

Luxury recovery as logistics.  
Ketamine therapy rooms, juice cleanses, and discreet transfers that move product, cash, and compromised people under the language of healing.  
The horror is how beautiful and persuasive the front is.

## Pilot engine

**She is not a dealer yet.**

Pilot opens inside a successful stay: ocean light, breathwork, a famous client “getting honest.”  
Our lead — **Elena** — is the staffer who actually keeps people from dying: dosing schedules, crisis nights, family NDAs.  
She finds a discrepancy (wrong ketamine lot / chauffeured “alumni transfer” / kitchen invoice that doesn’t match the private chef’s real deliveries).  
She reports it up… and gets promoted for her discretion.  
Pilot end: she realizes the clinic didn’t hide the crime from her — it **auditioned** her.

## Characters

- **Elena Varga** — clinical / sober-living director; crisis whisperer; moral center that corrodes
- **Dr. Marcus Hale** — celebrity psychiatrist / ketamine prophet; face of the brand; architect of the “protocol”
- **Lila Quinn** — sober influencer in residence; audience surrogate who monetizes recovery — and may already be couriering
- **Chef Anton** — private chef for mansion campuses; the kitchen is the real loading dock
- **“Mother” Carmen** — unseen-at-first logistics partner; speaks in philanthropy and flowers

## World

Dual-coast wellness corridor: Malibu oceanfront mansions + Miami Beach aftercare villas.  
Visual language: linen, jade rollers, salt rooms, amber vials, black SUVs with no logos, sunrise yoga over a hearse-equivalent of luxury vans, donor walls, NDAs as décor.  
Subculture: sober-famous, ketamine clinics, concierge medicine, private chefs, “alumni” networks that never graduate.

## Shonda hook

Mid-pilot: Elena saves a overdosing A-lister with a “proprietary rescue protocol” the clinic forbids staff from documenting.  
Everyone celebrates her.  
Floor drop: the “rescue meds” were never pharmacy stock — they were product pulled from the same pipeline the client was smuggling in through “wellness samples.” Saving him completed the delivery.

## Breaking Bad pivot

Elena doesn’t want empire. She wants fewer dead kids on her watch.  
Her competence (inventory discipline, crisis dosing, family management) is exactly what the operation lacked.  
She starts “fixing” the system to reduce harm — and invents a more efficient cartel clinic than the monsters who hired her.

## Season engine

Each season arc = a new “healing product line” that expands territory:

1. Ketamine / aftercare corridor (Malibu ↔ Miami)  
2. Celebrity relapse cycles as recurring logistics windows  
3. Sober-influencer soft launches as demand gen  
4. Philanthropy galas as laundering theater  
5. Rival “ethical” clinic war — purity language as turf war  

Episode rule: every solution that keeps a patient alive also deepens Elena’s fingerprints.

## Teaser rules

**Part 1 may show:** ocean mansions, breathwork, ketamine drips, celebrity soft arrivals, private chefs, serene beauty, Elena’s competence, one wrong vial / wrong invoice.

**Part 2 may show:** black vans, ledger panic, influencer livestream lies, a body treated like a “transfer,” first taste of real enforcement — still through wellness aesthetics (raid on a yoga deck > alley shootout).
